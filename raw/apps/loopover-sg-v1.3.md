# Loopover: independent rows and columns

## In three points
- **What:** A Loopover sliding-tile puzzle: each drag shifts one row or one column, and tiles that leave one edge come back on the other. Grids from 3 x 3 to 8 x 8, numbers or colors only, coloring by column, row, diagonal, antidiagonal or coordinates, timed solves with best time and fewest moves.
- **So what:** It is plain Loopover, where only the dragged line moves. The page names its source and its limit: the layout and colors follow a screenshot of Quantum Loopover v1.37 at superliminal.com, and it does not show linked moves, where shifting one line also shifts another.
- **Now what:** Play it; it is not a water app. Best scores are kept in the browser, separately for each coloring.

This summary was written after the fact. The page ships no technical summary of its own. Its version is the page's own ("v1.3"). The file name above is the Netlify site's. Everything below is the page's. Nothing was run to write this file.

## What it does

Put the numbers back in reading order with 1 at the top left. Drag a tile sideways to slide its row, up or down to slide its column. Diagonal colorings wrap around the edges the way the moves do; Coordinates mixes each tile's color from its home position (red rises with the column, green with the row, some blue for dark tiles near the top left). With Colors only, the board is solved when it matches the small goal picture. Keyboard: arrows move the highlight, Shift + arrow or W A S D slides, Z undoes.

## What this does not show

As the page says: any linked moves, where shifting one line also shifts another (the "quantum" variant it takes its look from).
