# Water Modeling to 3000: a forecast to 2050 and dream time to the year 3000, with one check all the way through

## In three points
- **What:** Bob's timeline of water modeling from a baseline of 12 October 2026 to the year 3000, on a compressed log time scale: a forecast to 2050 in tiers that say how much weight each horizon can bear (forecast, reasoned guess, speculation), then "dream time" from 2075 to 3000. Prompted by @ashebytes on dream time; also an 11-post X thread.
- **So what:** Each horizon carries a check, and the check is the thread: continuity in every .rpt is the first gate today, conformance to a written specification by a public test corpus later, and in the year 3000 "someone still checks the continuity error". The page's line is that engines come and go and invariants travel; what reaches 3000 is the habit of checking.
- **Now what:** Read it as an argument for the 2030 test suite: the page names four things that set the pace of the forecast (EPA's decision on SWMM6, how few core developers carry the engine, who funds maintenance, and whether a public corpus with a register of expected differences becomes the shared definition of SWMM). Everything after 2026 is a projection.

## What it shows

- Baseline, 12 October 2026: SWMM6 is an alpha, with a stable release targeted for 2027 ("a target, not a commitment"); the engine is thread-safe and handle-based, with pip-installable Python bindings and an MCP server; 2D finite-volume overland flow has shipped in the 6.0 alphas, 1D finite-volume routing is in development code; SWMM5 is still what consultants and regulators run.
- +4 years (forecast): 6.x stable, with parity to SWMM5 shown on a public corpus and a published register of expected differences; dynamic wave and finite volume side by side; agents run, diff and diagnose models routinely.
- +9 years (reasoned guess): uncertainty bands by default; the .inp becomes an export format; SWMM5 frozen as the regulatory reference while SWMM6 earns acceptance. Bob adds a firsthand historical note: in the 1990s SWMM was supported outside USEPA.
- +14 to +24 years (speculation): agents build and calibrate models; any engine that conforms to the written specification counts as SWMM; the live twin becomes city infrastructure and permits are checked against it.
- Dream time, +49 to +974 years: the network computes itself; the programmable catchment; one water at any gravity; hydrology run backwards; "no floods, only choreography".

## Checks

The page is a forecast and a speculation, labelled by tier; it reports no measurements. Its own check at each horizon is listed with the horizon.

## What this does not show

The page says everything after 2026 is a projection and that its tiers say how much weight each horizon can bear. A continuity check is necessary, not sufficient: a model can close its mass balance and still miss the peak, the timing or the flood extent.
