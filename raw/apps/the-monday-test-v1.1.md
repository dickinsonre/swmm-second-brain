# The Monday Test: nine experiences for SWMM5, ICM and SWMM6 modelers

## In three points
- **What:** Nine short "journeys" for SWMM5, InfoWorks ICM and SWMM6 modelers, each built on a finding from Bob's own measured work and walked through Jeff Bullas's six-stage Transformation Economy arc (surprise, understand, choose, act, reflect, transform), ending in a Monday card: what you will do differently on Monday. Examples: "The sealed lawn" (MaxInfil = 0.5 in or 50 in, the lawn seals either way); "Two missing lines" (same .inp, two builds, 64.31 cfs and 24.69 cfs); "Exit code zero" (the run failed and the process says it did not); "HYDGRAD, not gradient" (ICM field names are case-sensitive).
- **So what:** The app does not let the frame vouch for the claims: its Evidence table has one row per claim with the engine, build, date and what the claim does not cover. Of 23 claims, 12 were run on a compiled engine or a real script for this app on 27 September 2026, 3 were exercised against a stub of ICM, and 8 are cited from earlier work and not re-run.
- **Now what:** Use it to turn a finding into a habit. It does not show that any finding holds on your models, your builds or your ICM version; that is what the Monday kits are for.

This summary was written after the fact. The page ships no technical summary of its own. Its version and date are the page's own ("v1.1, 20 Oct 2026"). The file name above is ours. Every number below is copied from the page. Nothing was run to write this file.

## What it does

Tabs: Journeys (filter by SWMM5, ICM, SWMM6), Monday card, Evidence, The frame, Doc. Four in-browser .inp tools, a hand-rolled zip writer for the Monday kits, card exports, a help tour and a walkthrough recorder. Other journeys: "respond_to? says yes" (the method is there, calling it is not allowed); "The script is not where you saved it" (require_relative looks beside a temporary copy); "One file, two engines" (nine lines, nine fatal errors, exit code 0).

## Checks (as stated on the page)

The Evidence table's census: engine-verified 9, source read and engine-confirmed 2, tested 1, stub-verified 3, from Bob's record 3, from his record and not re-run 5.

## What this does not show

As the page states: that any finding holds on your models, builds or ICM version.
