# BobSWMM App Catalog: the Multiverse Educational Lab catalog of Bob's apps in one file, with a health check

## In three points
- **What:** Bob's catalog of his own small web apps (BobSWMM, also called the Multiverse Educational Lab), rebuilt from its Lovable source as one HTML file: 106 records, 364 wires, with the finder, toolkits, workflows, a map, a glossary and a Health sheet.
- **So what:** Counting it found drift the original shows as fact: 18 records flagged hidden (6 still in a Math Curiosities drawer, 12 nowhere), and five hero tools with "Used 32 times this week"-style counts that are typed into the code as simulated.
- **Now what:** Check the Health sheet before trusting a number from the original site. No person has reviewed the findings against the live original.

This summary was written after the fact. The page ships no technical summary of its own. Its version and date are the page's own ("v1.0 · 2026-10-21"). The file name above is ours. Every number below is copied from the page. Nothing was run to write this file.

## What it does

The catalog holds 88 apps on the main shelf (SWMM5, InfoWorks ICM, hydrology and tools), 6 math curiosities, and 12 older apps kept in the data but hidden. The original lives at bobswmmapps.lovable.app. Tabs: Catalog, Toolkits, Workflows, Map, Compare, Glossary, Health, About, Doc. The About tab carries Bob's bio and the photo the original site uses.

## Results the page reports

- 106 records, 364 wires.
- 18 records carry hidden: true; 6 still appear in the Math Curiosities drawer, 12 appear nowhere.
- Five hero tools show lines such as "Used 32 times this week", typed into the code under a comment reading "Simulated weekly usage counts".
- The finder expands words through a synonym table; for a conversion request, XP to SWMM Bridge, the tool built for the job, ranks 4th behind InfoSewer To ICM Guide. The Catalog tab shows each score and its parts.

## Checks (as stated on the page)

- Counts and findings are checked by a node test and an independent Python audit; every finding quotes its source lines.

## What this does not show

- The original's single app review is marked in its source as AI-generated.
