# SWMM Futurescape: a design-fiction archive of EPA SWMM in 2030, with every claim labelled

## In three points
- **What:** A design-fiction archive: the 2024 encyclopedia page on EPA SWMM, four imagined 2030 versions of it (AI-native, climate-resilient, digital twin, open science), and the counter-scenarios they leave out. Every claim carries a label saying whether you can check it today. A single-file rebuild of Bob's Lovable project; the scenario text was written 23 July 2026, which stays the factual cutoff for scenarios A to D.
- **So what:** The Lovable page's box "Verified status as of July 23, 2026" made six claims; re-checked 94 days later, on 25 October 2026, three held (EPA SWMM 5.2.4 is the newest EPA release; SWMM-CAT maintenance ended 2025-03-18; CMIP7 advancing), one was superseded (OpenSWMM 6.0.0a2 as the latest alpha), and two were overstated when written (NOAA Atlas 15 "in publication"; Atlas 15 supersedes Atlas 14). The edition lists 22 changes in all: 11 content corrections, 7 code repairs, 3 additions and 1 unit conversion.
- **Now what:** Use it to argue about where SWMM is going, with fiction and fact kept apart. The four 2030 pages are imagined; only the labelled observations can be checked, and facts in an "Observed" box have a shelf life.

This summary was written after the fact. The page ships no technical summary of its own. Its version and date are the page's own ("v2.0 · 2026-10-25"). The file name above is ours. Every number below is copied from the page. Nothing was run to write this file.

## What it does

Tabs: 2024 Baseline, A AI-Native, B Climate-Resilient, C Digital Twin, D Open Science, Counter-scenarios, About, Doc. Green tags in the text (such as E1) link to the edition notes; Export saves the notes as CSV. Examples of the corrections: three empty placeholder figures replaced by a drawn diagram of SWMM's four compartments (atmosphere, land surface, groundwater, transport) (E2); a history table that jumped from SWMM 4 to SWMM 1 now has rows for SWMM 3 (1979 to 1981, UF and CDM) and SWMM 2 (1973 to 1975, UF) (E3); SWMM 1's developers corrected from "UF, CDM, M&E" to "M&E, UF, WRE" after the 1971 EPA report (E4).

## Checks (as stated on the page)

- Six status claims re-checked with their owners on 25 October 2026: 3 held, 1 superseded, 2 overstated.
- Each of the 22 edition notes gives what was there, what is there now, why, the source and a confidence.

## What this does not show

The 2030 pages are design fiction, written 23 July 2026 and not updated past that cutoff; the labels say what can be checked today, not what will happen.
