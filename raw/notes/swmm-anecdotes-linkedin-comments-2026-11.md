# SWMM anecdotes from the comments on a LinkedIn post, November 2026

**Status:** seed
**Date:** 2026-11-15

## What this file is

Bob pasted the comment thread under one of his own LinkedIn posts into a Claude session on 15 November 2026 and said it "might be good to add to the repo as anecdotes of SWMM". The comments were between eleven minutes and one day old. Claude (Anthropic) typed this note from that paste. Bob has not yet read this file.

Kept: Bob's own comments, in full where they bear on SWMM, and the sentences of other people's public comments that recall SWMM history or their own modeling work, word for word, with the name each was posted under.

Left out, because this repository is public: comments that only congratulate, remarks about anyone's family or private life, job titles, and the reaction and impression counts. The post itself was not in the paste; its text, date and link are not here.
