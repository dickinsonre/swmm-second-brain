# Unhappy with some components of the LI Social Selling Index, I make up my own SSI+ Index

![Unhappy with some components of the LI Social Selling Index, I make up my own Index ](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQHFTQlufa6hAw "Unhappy with some components of the LI Social Selling Index, I make up my own Index  ")

I've been utilizing LinkedIn's SSI score for over a decade, yet I've noticed two components of my score—finding people and adding insights—rarely change. Here’s a snapshot of my current SSI components:

- **Establish your professional brand:** 23.5

- **Find the right people:** 23.38 now was 11.69

- **Engage with insights:** 22.52 now was 11.26

- **Build relationships:** 25

**New total score:** 94.40 from 71

Despite growing my following from 5,000 to 17,000 and accumulating 15,000 connections over the past ten years, my "Find the right people" score remains static. Interestingly, it has even decreased since joining Autodesk three years ago. Many of my new connections now use Forma, Civil 3D, Revit, and InfraWorks, rather than SWMM5, but they are still valuable global coworkers.

Moreover, my "Engage with insights" score hasn’t reflected the increased engagement I’ve experienced. My reactions and impressions have grown tenfold since 2015, indicating significant improvement. My comments, whether seen as silly or insightful, are generating much more activity.

Consequently, I've devised my own SSI+ index. In this custom index, I double the scores of the middle two components while keeping the others unchanged. This adjustment gives me a more accurate score (to me) of 94 instead of 71. This score fluctuates based on my activity, providing a better indicator—showing dips to 92 or rises to 95, which I find more reflective of my LinkedIn presence.
