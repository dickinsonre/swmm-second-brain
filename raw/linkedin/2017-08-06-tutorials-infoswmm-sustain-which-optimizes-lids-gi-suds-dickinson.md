# Tutorials for InfoSWMM Sustain  Which Optimizes for #LIDs, #GI, #SuDS, #WSuDS on Small Runoff Surfaces

![](https://media.licdn.com/mediaC5112AQHCcFsmi4ypYA)

InfoSWMM Sustain is a software tool designed for use by professionals in the field of watershed and stormwater engineering. It is specifically designed to perform sophisticated hydrologic and water quality modeling in watersheds and urban streams. The software enables users to develop, evaluate, and select the most effective and cost-efficient combinations of best management practices (BMPs), Low Impact Development (LID) techniques, and Sustainable Urban Drainage Systems (SUDS) for different watershed scales.

One of the most important things about InfoSWMM Sustain is that it can model how different BMPs, LID techniques, and SUDS affect the watershed's hydrology and water quality. By looking at the costs and effectiveness of different combinations of these practices, users can figure out which ones are best for a certain watershed.

InfoSWMM Sustain can model hydrology and water quality, and it also has a number of other useful features, such as the ability to see and analyze the results of a model, make reports and graphs, and do sensitivity analyses. Overall, InfoSWMM Sustain is a powerful tool that helps watershed and stormwater engineering professionals better understand the complex interactions between land use, hydrology, and water quality in urban and rural watersheds.
