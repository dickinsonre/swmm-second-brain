- **3/ Why You Should Not Import InfoSewer to InfoSWMM but Use the Newer AWI Ruby/ODIC Methodology with SQL from the Autodesk Water Infrastructure GitHub**: This advises against direct InfoSewer to InfoSWMM imports, recommending the advanced AWI Ruby/ODIC methodology with SQL from Autodesk’s GitHub for better integration and efficiency.

- **4/ Navigating the Landscape of Hydraulic Modeling: A Comparative Study of InfoSewer, ICM InfoWorks, and ICM SWMM for Steady State Models**: A comparative analysis of InfoSewer, ICM InfoWorks, and ICM SWMM is presented, focusing on their capabilities for steady-state hydraulic modeling.

- **5/ Running List of my LinkedIn Articles**: This section provides a catalog of the my LinkedIn articles, serving as a resource hub for further reading on related topics.

- **6/ InfoSewer Load Allocation Expertise: Test Yourself with 50 Questions for 2025 to 2050**: It offers a set of 50 questions to test expertise in InfoSewer load allocation, aimed at professionals planning for future decades.
