### 🎯 The Four Dimensions We Measure

After years of working with InfoWorks ICM professionals, I noticed something fascinating: we all approach modelling differently. Some of us obsess over every manhole invert level, while others focus on the big-picture system behavior. Some automate everything with Ruby scripts, others prefer hands-on control. **So I built something fun:** an interactive personality quiz that identifies your unique InfoWorks ICM modelling style. Here are the four dimensions: Approach, Workflow, Solving Style and Data Quality.

**1. Modeling Approach** — Are you Detail-Focused (every lateral, gully, and connection) or Big-Picture (strategic simplifications, trunk sewers only)?

**2. Workflow Style** — Do you embrace Automation (Ruby scripts, batch processing) or prefer Manual control at every step?

**3. Problem Solving** — Are you Systematic (documented procedures, checklists) or Intuitive (pattern recognition, experience-guided)?

**4. Data Quality** — Are you a Perfectionist (zero tolerance for gaps) or Pragmatic (practical trade-offs to meet deadlines)?
