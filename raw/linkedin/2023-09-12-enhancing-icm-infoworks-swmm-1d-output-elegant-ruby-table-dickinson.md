> **Introduction**

The Ruby code for ICM SWMM and ICM InfoWorks statistics for nodes, links and subcatchments are located on the [Innovyze GitHub](https://github.com/innovyze/Open-Source-Support/tree/main/01%20InfoWorks%20ICM/01%20Ruby/02%20SWMM) and are contained in folders 41 to 45.
