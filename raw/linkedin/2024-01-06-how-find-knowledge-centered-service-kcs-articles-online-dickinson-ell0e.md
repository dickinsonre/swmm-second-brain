# How to Find Knowledge-Centered Service (KCS) Articles in the Online ICM Help File for InfoSewer, InfoSWMM and XPSWMM Conversions

![Green Infrastructure in the future ](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQEDvlDPxhvCEg "Green Infrastructure in the future ")

As part of our ongoing technological evolution and in response to user needs, we are transitioning from our legacy software platforms, XPSWMM, InfoSewer, and InfoSWMM, to the more advanced Autodesk products, I**CM Standard and ICM Ultimate**. This shift is not universally known but is crucial for our user community.

To facilitate this transition for our diverse user base, we have implemented direct import capabilities for InfoSWMM to an ICM SWMM network and for XPSWMM XPX files to either ICM InfoWorks or ICM SWMM. This ensures a smooth migration of data and models, preserving the integrity and accuracy of existing projects.

For users migrating from InfoSewer to ICM SWMM and ICM InfoWorks, we have developed Ruby and ODIC CFG files, which are readily available on our Github repository. To access these resources, users can visit the [ICM Technical Information Hub](https://boards.autodesk.com/4bf242?token=a671af9e76), which provides essential links, GitHub codes, and comprehensive Readme files with detailed explanations and instructions.

Recognizing the need for thorough guidance through this transition, we have also compiled an extensive knowledge base. Approximately 100 Knowledge Center Service (KCS) articles are available to date, offering step-by-step guidance on importing to ICM. These articles are a valuable resource for users seeking detailed instructions and best practices.

To access these articles, users can simply link to the ICM Online help file and perform searches for terms like 'InfoSewer', 'InfoSWMM', or 'XPSWMM'. This will direct them to a wealth of articles, each tailored to address specific aspects of the import process and to clarify any complexities encountered during the transition.

This comprehensive suite of resources and tools is part of our commitment to ensuring a seamless and efficient transition for all our users, enabling them to leverage the advanced capabilities of ICM Standard and ICM Ultimate effectively.

The image below show how easy this is to find.  If you search for InfoSWMM or InfoSewer, you will see the links under Technical Support.  There are now 26 for InfoSewer and 23 for InfoSWMM.
