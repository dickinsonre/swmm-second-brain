# My 1991 @UF President of the Mayor's Council Certificate #SWMM 2,3,4

![](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQGE7MueB1gZVA)

Diamond Village. UF - University of Florida - Gainesville Florida

1/ On the hunt for books I recently came across an award that @UF presented to me in 1991 for my many years of service as President of the Mayor's Council. Members of the Mayor's Council represent the heads of UF's Married Housing Complexes. From 1985 to 1991, I served as Mayor of Diamond Village.
