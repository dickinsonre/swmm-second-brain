### A fast, local search engine for the EPA Storm Water Management Model (SWMM) 5.2 manual documentation that has 630 sample files and runs PySWMM

Built by \[Robert Dickinson\]([https://www.linkedin.com/in/robertdickinson/](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robertdickinson/)) — Data source: \[Scott Jeffers' SWMM Manual Collection\]([https://swmm-manual.netlify.app/](https://swmm-manual.netlify.app/))

> **What the App Does**

This app crawls the entire SWMM 5.2 Manual Collection — **5 separate manuals**, **53 chapters**, **621 sections**, and **248,000+ words** — and combines them into a single, instant-search interface. Instead of clicking through 5 different manual sidebars, engineers type a term and get results from everywhere in under a second.

![](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/v2/D4E12AQHzEJJF73jndw/article-inline_image-shrink_1500_2232/B4EZ5wv.ErIkAQ-/0/1780008084857?e=1795651200&v=beta&t=LTEc0ytkW3cc3CywKXHDaGfQkRJk-syeghPvoUu1kY4)

> **App Details**

**SWMM5 Manual Search** is a fast, all-in-one reference and learning platform for the EPA Storm Water Management Model (SWMM) 5.2. At its core is a millisecond full-text search engine indexing all 621 sections and ~248,000 words of the official SWMM manual, with fuzzy "did-you-mean" matching, 70+ engineering synonyms, chapter-scoped filtering, and advanced query syntax. It also bundles a complete toolkit for stormwater engineers: a 1,412-term auto-extracted glossary, 43 interactive diagrams (hydrology, hydraulics, LID, routing, and water quality), 30 hydraulic calculators (Manning, Rational, Green-Ampt, culvert headwater, hydraulic jump, pump operating point, and more).

It also has 630 INP files—each with US/SI units and copy-to-clipboard results), a field-by-field INP file reference, an INP quality checker, and an ICM/InfoWorks variable-mapping table. It can generate runnable .inp files for any of the 621 manual sections and execute them server-side against the genuine EPA SWMM 5.2.4 engine (via pyswmm), returning the full report with a continuity-error verdict. A built-in AI assistant answers questions using retrieval over the manual, and the whole app works offline as a PWA, supports dark mode, deep linking, and a public REST API so other SWMM tools can tap into the same knowledge base. Created by Robert Dickinson, with manual content stewardship by Scott Jeffers, PE, PhD. Scott used the well-known EPA SWMM reference manuals authorwed primarily by the esteemed Lew Rossman of the USEPA.
