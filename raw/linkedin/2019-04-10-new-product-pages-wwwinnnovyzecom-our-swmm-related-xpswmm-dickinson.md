# New Product Pages at www.innnovyze.com for our SWMM related Products, InfoSWMM, XPSWMM, InfoSewer, and InfoWorks ICM

![](https://media.licdn.com/mediaC4D12AQHh8cCIiU2HJA)

Obviously, with my background in SWMM, I am partial to SWMM software and we have 4 good platforms for SWMM or partial SWMM modeling. InfoSewer and InfoWorks ICM have SWMM components,

A combination of a few of my favorite things (1) the great looking @Innovyze website, (2) InfoSWMM, (3) SWMM, (4) ESRI and (5) Innovyze's PM Team, InfoSWMM \| Urban Drainage and Sewer Modeling - Innovyze https://www.innovyze.com/en-us/products/infoswmm … " Geospatial Urban Drainage Modeling"

XPSWMM \| 1D/2D Modeling Package for Floodplains, Stormwater and Sanitary Systems - Innovyze [https://www.innovyze.com/en-us/products/xpswmm …](https://t.co/b4uQnjzBVE) Holistic is a good description!

Hydraulic and Hydrologic Modeling \| InfoWorks ICM has a lot of SWMM components [https://www.innovyze.com/en-us/products/infoworks-icm …](https://t.co/pNXt8peZrB)

InfoSewer \| Urban Sanitary Systems Modeling - Innovyze [https://www.innovyze.com/en-us/products/infosewer …](https://t.co/EtiT4To8HE) InfoSewer is a 1D sewer modeling software used for capacity analysis and planning of gravity sewer systems, pumps and force mains.
