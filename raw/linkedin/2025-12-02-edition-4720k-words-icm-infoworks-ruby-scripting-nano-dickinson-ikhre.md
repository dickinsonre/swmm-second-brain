# Edition 47—20K Words on ICM InfoWorks Ruby Scripting, Nano Banana Diagram Version

![](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/v2/D4E12AQHBow7-cAKBmw/series-logo_image-shrink_300_300/series-logo_image-shrink_300_300/0/1707065394509?e=1795651200&v=beta&t=tN-hhC1z9uquar5xA2f4iKHMUJauOcieSynf04B5KXc)

### EPASWMM5 Related Software Five decades with SWMM, still shipping code. SWMM5/6, ICM with Ruby, browser apps that teach the engines — and the art

![ InfoWorks Exchange API documentation](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQG0JO0T_6cvag " InfoWorks Exchange API documentation")

You may have been seeing very detailed diagrams of engineering concepts here on LinkedIn and on X or Twitter. They are often or mostly made by Nano Banana Pro, which is part of Gemini 3.0. I like it, as it gives me a fresh perspective on ICM InfoWorks and SWMM5. As I also like doing Ruby Scripts for ICM InfoWorks and ICM SWMM Networks, I thought it would be interesting (to me) to make Nano Banana ICM Ruby Diagrams. Since I made more than 20 diagrams of them, and a picture is worth a thousand words or more, I will call this a 20K article on ICM Ruby. The above image is a general diagram of the ICM Ruby API.

The source of these diagrams is the wonderful ICM InfoWorks online [help file from Autodesk](https://help.autodesk.com/view/IWICMS/2026/ENU/?guid=Innovyze_Exchange_Introduction_ICM_introduction_html). It is the section that starts with InfoWorks Exchange (as shown below). You can also get all of the open-source Ruby scripts from our [GitHub repository.](https://github.com/innovyze/Open-Source-Support)
