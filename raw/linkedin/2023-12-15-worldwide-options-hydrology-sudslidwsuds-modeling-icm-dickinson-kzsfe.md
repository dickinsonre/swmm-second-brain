# Worldwide Options for Hydrology for SuDS/LID/WSUD Modeling in InfoWorks ICM Ultimate or Standard

![A very intense Green House](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQFPjvpQsTlCjA "A very intense Green House")

Imagine your house, surrounded by biofiltration cells, rain gardens, a few swales, rain barrels to collect runoff, a green roof, an infiltration trench, and a permeable pavement driveway.  You work at Autodesk, so your house can be anywhere. ICM (Integrated Catchment Model) accounts for this location uncertainty by having 16 different ways to estimate runoff, 19 ways to estimate infiltration, and 17 ways to input rainfall spatially over your network. If you live in Europe, Asia, Australia, or North America you have local options for modeling the hydrology of your house.
