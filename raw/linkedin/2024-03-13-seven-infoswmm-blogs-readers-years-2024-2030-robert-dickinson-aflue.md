### EPASWMM5 Related Software Five decades with SWMM, still shipping code. SWMM5/6, ICM with Ruby, browser apps that teach the engines — and the art

![Seven InfoSWMM Blogs for Readers in the Years 2024-2030+](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQErA-IQEAkOwA "Seven InfoSWMM Blogs for Readers in the Years 2024-2030+")

This is for present and future readers. It contains seven long blogs from [swmm5.org](http://swmm5.org) about the inner workings of InfoSWMM. I will add that the InfoSWMM engine is now called OneSWMM and has been modified to meet the needs of the Autodesk Water Infrastructure InfoDrainage and ICM SWMM programs. The key point to remember is that InfoSWMM and InfoSewer have all their model data in the ISDB and IEDB folders, respectively, and the model data is readable using Excel and other programs. Open data source, and we have help in the ICM Help file as KCS articles to help you convert, as well as GitHub Ruby, CFG, and SQL code for the conversion. InfoSWMM also has a one-scenario-at-a-time importer in ICM.

The articles form the backbone of the newsletter. Seven articles make up One Newsletter edition. Once 19 editions are published, or approximately every 133 articles, there will be a summary edition. The far-reaching goal is 1729 articles, 247 editions, and 20 summary editions.

**Why 1729:** The number 1729 has **8 factors**, which are 1, 7, 13, 19, 91, 133, 247, and 1729. A bit of history about 1729: It's famously known as the **Hardy-Ramanujan number** after a story involving two great mathematicians, G.H. Hardy and Srinivasa Ramanujan. According to the anecdote, Hardy visited Ramanujan in the hospital and mentioned that he arrived in a taxi numbered 1729, which he found to be a rather uninteresting number. Ramanujan immediately responded that 1729 is very interesting because it is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two cubes in two different ways: 1729=1^3+12^3=9^3+10^3. This property makes 1729 a significant figure in mathematics, showcasing Ramanujan's extraordinary intuitive grasp of numbers.
