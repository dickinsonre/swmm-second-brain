# Eight ICMs in One. ICM Ultimate, Sewer, Flood, and Viewer for InfoWorks and SWMM Networks.

![Eight ICMs in One. ICM Ultimate, Sewer, Flood, and Viewer for InfoWorks and SWMM Networks.](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQFkeHoT-plJDQ "Eight ICMs in One. ICM Ultimate, Sewer, Flood, and Viewer for InfoWorks and SWMM Networks.")

A note from the Autodesk Water Lifecycle team: the morphing of ICM Standard into ICM Flood and ICM Sewer and the continuing awesomeness of ICM Ultimate. I wanted to mention here how ICM Ultimate is actually 4 ICMs in one (if you purchase ICM Ultimate and 8 if you double count InfoWorks and SWMM networks in ICM). You can see more detail in the outstanding One Water Blog from Autodesk Water

[One Water Blog](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/louise-cramp-350830222_meet-the-new-flexible-options-for-infoworks-activity-7337835925319671811-aQd0?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAAEAvcwBVte4NjFU9EyFPNigxyghgnrHf9c)

You can change what you see by setting a flag in the shortcut properties

"C:\Program Files\Autodesk\InfoWorks ICM Sewer 2026\InfoWorksICM.exe" /ADSK:Sewer

"C:\Program Files\Autodesk\InfoWorks ICM Sewer 2026\InfoWorksICM.exe" /ADSK:Flood

"C:\Program Files\Autodesk\InfoWorks ICM Sewer 2026\InfoWorksICM.exe" /ADSK:Viewer

"C:\Program Files\Autodesk\InfoWorks ICM Sewer 2026\InfoWorksICM.exe" /ADSK:Ultimate
