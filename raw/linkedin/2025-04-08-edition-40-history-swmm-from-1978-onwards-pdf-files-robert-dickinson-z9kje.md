**One-Sentence Summary**: Edition 40 traces the history of SWMM from 1978 through its evolution from punch cards to AI, detailing key manuals, verification processes, and modern comparisons like SWMM5 versus InfoWorks ICM, while preserving historical test files.

**One-Paragraph Summary**: Edition 40 chronicles the history of the Stormwater Management Model (SWMM) from 1978, beginning with the author’s punch-card experiences and progressing through magnetic tapes, PCs, and AI, as shared in slides from the FSAWWA seminar in Tampa. It covers foundational documents like the SWMM1 Final Report, Verification and Testing, and Program Listing, alongside manuals for SWMM2, SWMM3 (in two parts), and SWMM4’s runoff, transport, storage treatment, stats, rain, and temp blocks. The edition also includes Extran 3 and Extran 4 manuals, with SWMM5’s QA/QC manual incorporating their test files, and features a modern comparison of SWMM5 and InfoWorks ICM hydraulic networks using the Bill James Similarity Index at ICWMM2025. The closing note expresses gratitude to readers, emphasizing the articles’ value as situational resources in the evolving field of stormwater modeling.
