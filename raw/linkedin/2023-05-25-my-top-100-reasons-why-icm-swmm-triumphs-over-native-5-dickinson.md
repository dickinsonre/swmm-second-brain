# My Top 100 Reasons: Why ICM SWMM Triumphs Over Native SWMM 5, IMO - based on My 24-Year Voyage with EPASWMM5

![](https://media.licdn.com/mediaD4E12AQF59zX-VZw0mQ)

> **My Top 100 Reasons:** Why ICM SWMM Triumphs Over SWMM 5 - Insights from My 24-Year Voyage with EPASWMM5 and almost 50 years with SWMM in General

I wanted to have more than the traditional top ten, so here are 💯 reasons it pays to use ICM in the form of its InfoWorks and SWMM networks for SWMM5 modeling. 🌐💧 From my end, there's a wealth of experience, having used ICM since 2010 📅 and EPASWMM5 from 2002+ 🕒. This journey has been filled with insights, breakthroughs, and a deep dive into the nuances of water management and simulation. 🤿🌊

The reasons are manifold, ranging from ICM's robust and flexible modeling capabilities 💪🔄, to its seamless integration with other systems 🤝🔗. Over the years, I've seen firsthand how ICM stands out for its advanced features, user-friendly interface, and ability to handle complex water networks with ease. 🧠🌐

So, here's to the countless benefits of using ICM and EPASWMM5 - a journey that's been as enlightening as it has been rewarding. 🎉📘 Each step has reinforced the importance of reliable water management solutions in our rapidly evolving world. 🌍💦
