SWMM5 and ICM InfoWorks

*The same network in two engines, with the Ruby scripts and apps that compare them.*

I spent 18 years as Product Sector Leader for InfoSWMM and InfoSewer at Innovyze, and I have written more than 440 Ruby scripts for InfoWorks ICM. This page shows how SWMM5 and ICM differ on the same network, and the scripts and apps that move models between them.
