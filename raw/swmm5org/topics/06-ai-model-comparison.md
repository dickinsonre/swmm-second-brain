Comparing Models with AI

*How to use AI to run, check and compare engines without fooling yourself.*

Over the past year I have built hundreds of apps with AI. The lesson that matters: AI makes comparisons cheap, so the checks have to be strict.

My rules for any comparison: the same input file, .out files compared byte for byte, and a null test before any claim that a change made a difference.
