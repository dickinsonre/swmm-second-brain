SWMM History: SWMM1 to SWMM6

*The engines from 1971 to now, rebuilt and running in your browser.*

SWMM began in 1969 to 1971 as an EPA project with Metcalf & Eddy, the University of Florida and Water Resources Engineers. I went to Florida in 1973 to work on it, and I have worked on SWMM itself since 1978, starting with SWMM 2.5.

This page collects that history and the old engines. SWMM 1 and EXTRAN III were rebuilt from their printed program listings, and SWMM 4.4H from its Fortran source, so you can run them in a browser next to SWMM5.
