The state of the wiki after 62 more app summaries and a note of their Netlify addresses were ingested on 17 November 2026, arranged by the six topics of [swmm5.org](entities/swmm5-org.md) [S06](sources/S06-swmm5org-topics-index.md). Two hundred sixty-seven of 503 raw files are ingested, three of them (the EPA hydraulics manual, the 2006 quality assurance report and the Rosetta Stone summary at version 2.6.2) in part. The wiki is deepest on SWMM's history, the insides of SWMM 5 and the translations of SWMM 5 and SWMM6; thin on ICM; and on SWMM6 it now holds measurements of the two solvers as well as the translations, though not their derivation.

## 1. SWMM history
- The timeline from 1969 to 2026 is on [SWMM version history, 1969 to 2026](concepts/swmm-version-history.md), with four places where Bob's own articles disagree with each other or with primary documents.
- [SWMM 1 (1971)](entities/swmm-1.md) is the best-documented subject in the wiki: 12,200 cards rebuilt and scored against six runs printed in 1971, with 14 faults found in the listing [S01](sources/S01-swmm1-engine-app-md.md). The method is on [Verification against printed runs](concepts/verification-against-printed-runs.md).
- [EXTRAN](entities/extran.md) now has its 1981 program in hand: the [EXTRAN III Workbench (app)](entities/extran-iii-workbench.md) runs it from the printed listing, matches 2,253 of 2,424 printed values exactly and fixes ten faults [S74](sources/S74-extran3-workbench-app-md.md). SWMM 4.4H's three later solvers are on [EXTRAN's three solvers: XROUTE, YROUTE, ZROUTE](concepts/extran-solvers-xroute-yroute-zroute.md) [S75](sources/S75-st-venant-rosetta-stone-app-md.md).
- Running today's models on yesterday's engines: [Converting SWMM 5 models for older engines](concepts/converting-swmm5-models-for-older-engines.md).
- [Block architecture and interface files](concepts/block-architecture.md) explains how SWMM 1 to 4 were put together and why that confused people later [S22](sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md).
- [Engine speed, 1988 to 2024](concepts/engine-speed-1988-2024.md): one test model, 5 minutes for 6 simulated hours in 1988 and 5 minutes for 36 simulated years in 2024 [S19](sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).
- People: [Robert (Bob) Dickinson](entities/robert-dickinson.md), [Wayne C. Huber](entities/wayne-huber.md), [Lewis A. (Lew) Rossman](entities/lew-rossman.md).
- Added 15 November 2026: [Anecdotes of SWMM](concepts/swmm-anecdotes.md), recollections in people's own words, the first of them from one of Bob's LinkedIn posts [S94](sources/S94-li-post-my-earlier-history-with-swmm.md) and the comments under it [S90](sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). They are memories and are marked as such. Bob has since dated the stay in Canberra, November 1991 to December 1992, and said the double pipes are what SWMM 5 calls barrels [S91](sources/S91-note-q64-answers-canberra-barrels-post-link.md).
- Added 17 November 2026: three apps run the old engines again. [SWMM 3 Runoff & Transport (app)](entities/swmm3-runoff-transport-app.md) rebuilds the 1981 Runoff and Transport blocks by subtraction from the SWMM 4.4 Fortran, because its page says no Version III listing is known to survive (but see Q102) [S193](sources/S193-swmm3-runoff-transport-summary.md). [SWMM 4 Twin Engines (app)](entities/swmm4-twin-engines.md) runs SWMM 4.4 with and without a Transport fix that takes one deck's printed continuity error from 56.180 % to 0.003 % [S194](sources/S194-swmm4-twin-engines-summary.md). [SWMM QA Report Bench (app)](entities/swmm-qa-report-bench.md) turns EPA's 2006 report into data and runs 5.2.4 beside it: 100 of 111 peaks within 5 % of the 2006 SWMM 5 figure [S192](sources/S192-swmm-qa-report-bench-app-md.md).

The manual says a pump's flow stays within its curve and also that a pump on a dry junction is set to the junction's inflow; which rule wins is Q62 [S60](sources/S60-epa-swmm5-reference-manual-vol2-hydraulics.md).
- Added 16 November 2026, from sections 1.2 to 1.5 of the same manual: [How SWMM 5 is organized: objects, state and the time loop](concepts/swmm5-objects-state-time-loop.md), with the four compartments, the objects, the state variables the manual calls "a surprisingly small number", and the time loop [S60](sources/S60-epa-swmm5-reference-manual-vol2-hydraulics.md).
- Added 16 November 2026, later, from Chapter 5 of the same manual: [Conduit cross-sections: the built-in shapes](concepts/conduit-cross-sections.md) and [Shape curves, transects and storage curves](concepts/shape-curves-transects-storage-curves.md). Every shape is a set of lookup tables or formulas for area, width, hydraulic radius and section factor; in a closed shape the Manning flow peaks before the conduit is full, at 0.92 to 0.9756 of the full area [S60](sources/S60-epa-swmm5-reference-manual-vol2-hydraulics.md).
- Graph theory, added 16 November 2026 from a third-party article, [Tivadar Danka](entities/tivadar-danka.md)'s "Graph Theory For Visual Learners" [S158](sources/S158-danka-graph-theory-for-visual-learners.md): six pages starting at [Graphs: nodes, edges, direction and weight](concepts/graph-basics.md). Where it meets the engine is on [Cycles and trees](concepts/graph-cycles-and-trees.md): kinematic wave accepts only directed acyclic networks, sorted by Kahn's algorithm, and stops on a loop [S60](sources/S60-epa-swmm5-reference-manual-vol2-hydraulics.md).
- Hydrology: [Runoff surfaces and subarea routing](concepts/runoff-surfaces-and-subarea-routing.md), [Subcatchment width](concepts/subcatchment-width.md), [Infiltration methods](concepts/infiltration-methods.md), [RDII unit hydrographs (RTK)](concepts/rdii-unit-hydrographs.md).
- Proposed changes to the engine: [Two-layer soil infiltration (SOIL2)](concepts/two-layer-infiltration.md), and five planned dynamic-wave fixes listed on [Dynamic wave routing](concepts/dynamic-wave-routing.md) [S55](sources/S55-swmm-brain-readme.md).
- A candidate finding from Bob's first template note: [Green-Ampt Newton test order](concepts/green-ampt-newton-test-order.md), a convergence test placed one line too early in `infil.c`; status "working", not rerun [S67](sources/S67-note-green-ampt-newton-test-order.md).
- LID and soils: [LID bio-retention layers](concepts/lid-bioretention-layers.md), [Topsoil over compacted clay](concepts/topsoil-over-compacted-clay.md), [Evaporation during rain (DRY_ONLY)](concepts/evaporation-during-rain.md), and the variant engine [swmm524-perched](entities/swmm524-perched.md). - Measured by Bob's apps in October and November 2026, none yet reviewed by a person: [The crown cap at mid-pipe](concepts/crown-cap-at-mid-pipe.md) [S75](sources/S75-st-venant-rosetta-stone-app-md.md); [Hot start: what a restart loses](concepts/hot-start-restart-state.md) [S79](sources/S79-teacher-forced-swmm-summary.md); [Run duration floored to whole seconds](concepts/run-duration-floor.md), met by two apps independently [S76](sources/S76-inside-the-network-cfd-app-md.md); and where SWMM 5 misses a control section, on [Control Section Lab (app)](entities/control-section-lab.md) [S80](sources/S80-control-section-lab-summary.md).

- [SWMM5 language translations](entities/swmm5-language-ports.md): EPA SWMM 5.2.4 in 19 languages, each byte-identical to EPA on every gate model it finished; 15 models stopped at the time limit in R and 2 each in Ruby and Perl [S41](sources/S41-swmm5-go-readme.md) [S54](sources/S54-swmm5-r-readme.md). ## Three things the first ingest showed
1. **Bob's published articles contradict each other on dates and credits.** Eighty-two items are open in [Open questions](open-questions.md); three were settled on 14 November by the EPA hydraulics manual, the first primary document ingested. The primary documents (the 1971 listing, EPA's update notes) settle some; others need Bob.
2. **Much of the article text is AI-written, and the newest articles say so while the older ones do not.** Each source page records how it was written. Claims that rest only on an AI-written passage are flagged.
3. **The strongest pages rest on the app .md files and the register, not on the articles.** Those sources carry procedures and numbers. The pattern argues for ingesting every app's .md before the remaining articles.
