**A caution about its source.** Everything on this page rests on [S102](../sources/S102-swmm-manual-search-landing-page-replit-summary.md), which Replit Agent wrote on 15 November 2026 from the project's source. Nothing here has been read by Bob (Q67).

**What the summary says** [S102](../sources/S102-swmm-manual-search-landing-page-replit-summary.md)
- The page links to https://sjswmm5manualsearch.com and shows a demo video, `swmm-video.mp4`, with an audio track, `carmen.mp3`.

- Three feature cards. The server registers no API routes.

- No license or attribution is recorded for the video and audio files.
