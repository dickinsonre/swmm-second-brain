Bob's catalog of his own published work in one searchable HTML file: newsletter editions, LinkedIn articles, posts and reposts, apps, tools, GitHub repositories, gists, blog posts, papers, OpenSWMM.org threads and LinkedIn comments. It answers "has Robert written or built something on X, and where is it?" Artifact v70, 14 November 2026 [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md).

**Where it came from.** It is a one-file copy of Bob's Replit app, NewsletterGallery, at `newsletter-gallery-robertdickinson.replit.app`, extended. The search, related-content and catalog ranking were ported from the Replit code and give identical results on the same data [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md).

The twelve rows sum to 2,533 (our arithmetic). The rest of the 9,964 are not itemized in the summary; [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md) says forum answers, LinkedIn comments and started threads were 73 percent of the records at v63. The forum figures match [OpenSWMM.org (the forum)](openswmm-forum.md) [S09](../sources/S09-openswmm-thread-list.md).

**How it dates things.** 66 newsletter dates and 163 article dates were read from LinkedIn on 10 November 2026. Posts and reposts are dated from the ID inside the LinkedIn link. Repository dates are last updates, not creation [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md).

**What it says about its own contents** [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md)
- Most summaries came from the Replit build and are AI-written; 38 articles added in November 2026 carry only their opening lines.
- Each tool card is labeled a code excerpt, a usage example or an outline. "None is labeled a working tool."
- No license is stated for the content.
- Human review: "none recorded for this build beyond Robert's own use of the page".

**Its link to this wiki.** Version 68 added this repository as a record, and v70 put a one-line link to it under the title. The summary says the library "links to it rather than copying it" [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md). The two do different jobs: the library says where a piece of work is; this wiki says what the work claims and which source backs it.

**A use for it here.** Its 163 dated articles and 66 editions are the list this wiki's LinkedIn queue can be checked against. That check has not been made.

**Scored by.** [Bob Usefulness Index (app)](bob-usefulness-index.md), which reads the library as evidence [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**Open.** The library counts 1,327 swmm5.org posts where [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md) counts 1,700 or more (Q54). See [Open questions](../open-questions.md) and [swmm5.org](swmm5-org.md).
