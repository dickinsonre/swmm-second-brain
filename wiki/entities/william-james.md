Engineer in computational hydraulics, author of Rules for Responsible Modeling, and the person Bob names with PCSWMM [S63](../sources/S63-li-edition-53-measure-of-honest-numbers.md). Professional record only.

**The book.** The app credits it as "William James, 2005: Rules for Responsible Modeling, 4th Edition", with a link to the original at chiwater.com [S62](../sources/S62-rules-for-responsible-modeling-app-md.md). Its subjects, as Bob lists them: reliability of input data, optimal complexity, sensitivity analysis, fuzzy logic, uncertainty disclosure, and the ethics of reporting a number that public money will be spent on [S63](../sources/S63-li-edition-53-measure-of-honest-numbers.md). See [Calibration, validation and uncertainty](../concepts/calibration-validation-uncertainty.md).

**Bob's judgment of him.** "Ahead of his time. Not ahead in the sense of having access to better technology. He had worse technology than almost anyone. Ahead in the sense of asking better questions." [S63](../sources/S63-li-edition-53-measure-of-honest-numbers.md)

**The app.** [Rules for Responsible Modeling (app)](rules-for-responsible-modeling-app.md) is Bob's interactive edition. Its chat, "Ask James", answers from summaries written for the app, not from verified book text, and its 17 chapter mappings have not been checked against the printed book by a person [S62](../sources/S62-rules-for-responsible-modeling-app-md.md).

**Not in the sources yet.** The book itself is not in `raw/`; it is a third-party work and would need Bob's say (CLAUDE.md, Long documents, rule 5). Nothing here says what PCSWMM was built from or who else built it. The 1983 date bears on Q31 in [Open questions](../open-questions.md).
