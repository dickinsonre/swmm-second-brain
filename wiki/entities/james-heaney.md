Of the University of Florida. Co-author of the SWMM 3 User's Manual and first author of the 1982 cost allocation paper with Bob [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md) [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md). Professional record only.

- With [Wayne C. Huber](wayne-huber.md), started on the stormwater software at the University of Florida in 1967, and was a co-author on SWMM 1, SWMM 2 and SWMM 3, in Bob's account [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md).
- SWMM 3 User's Manual: Huber, Heaney, Nix, Dickinson and Polmann, November 1981 [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).
- Had an office with Huber on the fourth floor of Black Hall [S12](../sources/S12-li-diamond-village.md), [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).
- Bob first heard him or Huber mention SWMM in an introductory environmental engineering course [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md). Bob dates their first meeting to 1976, in a water resources class Heaney taught, and their work together on game theory to 1977 [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md).
- "Methods for Apportioning the Cost of a Water Resource Project", Heaney and Dickinson, Water Resources Research 18(3), 476 to 482, June 1982. Bob says Heaney wrote it and made him co-author [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md). The paper is the reference for the 47 numeric comparisons in the [Cost Allocation Game Theory Explorer (app)](cost-allocation-explorer.md) [S61](../sources/S61-cost-allocation-explorer-app-md.md).
- The MCRS method is credited to Heaney and Dickinson, 1982 [S61](../sources/S61-cost-allocation-explorer-app-md.md). See [Cost allocation by cooperative game theory](../concepts/cost-allocation-methods.md).
- Built early expert systems in spreadsheets in the 1980s; Huber's tribute, as Bob quotes it, calls him "one of the earliest to become captivated by the possibility of Lotus 1-2-3 for solving engineering problems" [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md).
- His most-cited paper, per the article: "Robust water system design with commercial intelligent search optimizers", 1999. The citation counts beside it do not add up (Q37) [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md).

**To reconcile.** "Starting in 1967" [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md) sits two years before the 1969 start the other sources give for SWMM (see [SWMM version history, 1969 to 2026](../concepts/swmm-version-history.md)). It may be the year the two arrived at the university; the article does not say (Q41 in [Open questions](../open-questions.md)).
