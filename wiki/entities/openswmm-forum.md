**Ideas from a list thread, tested.** The [Topsoil Recovery Check (app)](topsoil-recovery-check.md) ran four ideas from replies on the SWMM-USERS list at OpenSWMM.org as its Experiment H: evaporation during rain, depression storage as the topsoil bowl, how long an infiltration test must run, and a curb-cut drain, which found defect D2 [S59](../sources/S59-topsoil-recovery-check-app-md.md). See [Evaporation during rain (DRY_ONLY)](../concepts/evaporation-during-rain.md) and [LID bio-retention layers](../concepts/lid-bioretention-layers.md).
