**What the summary says** [S295](../sources/S295-water-modeling-to-3000-summary.md)
- Baseline: SWMM6 is an alpha with a stable release targeted for 2027, "a target, not a commitment"; 2D finite-volume overland flow has shipped in the 6.0 alphas; SWMM5 is what consultants and regulators run.
- Bob's firsthand note: during the 1990s SWMM was supported outside USEPA.
- Four things set the pace: whether SWMM6 becomes the official EPA version, how few core developers carry the engine, who funds maintenance, and whether a public corpus with a register of expected differences becomes the shared definition of SWMM.
- Its line: engines come and go, invariants travel; a model can close its mass balance and still miss the peak, the timing or the flood extent.

**What it does not show.** Everything after 2026 is a projection; the tiers say how much weight each horizon can bear. [S295](../sources/S295-water-modeling-to-3000-summary.md)
