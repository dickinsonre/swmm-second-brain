**Positions he has taken**
- One subcatchment width for pervious and impervious subareas is wrong in principle [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md). See [Subcatchment width](../concepts/subcatchment-width.md).
- SWMM's openness is what matters most: "you have direct access to the source code so you can verify the computational algorithms" [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md).
- Lew Rossman's SWMM 5 documentation is the best software documentation in the world [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md).
- "The model is never the problem": the problem is the person who selects the data, chooses the complexity and interprets the output [S63](../sources/S63-li-edition-53-measure-of-honest-numbers.md). See [Calibration, validation and uncertainty](../concepts/calibration-validation-uncertainty.md) and Q40.
- Tacit knowledge "may be quantified, I hope" [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md).

**Disagreements between his own articles** (see [Open questions](../open-questions.md))
- Q05. [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md) says both that he led the CRADA that produced SWMM 5 and that the CDM team was led by Ted Burgess. The 2008 announcement quoted in [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md) calls him "the principal developer" of SWMM5, while [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md) and [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md) name Lew Rossman as its architect and author.
- Q06. [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md) says he has been "inside this story since 1980" and, a few lines on, working with SWMM "since 1978". The anniversary counts in [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md) were updated in different years. [S55](../sources/S55-swmm-brain-readme.md) puts his first model run in 1974; [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md) has him an undergraduate that year and on punch cards from 1977.
- Q29. [S55](../sources/S55-swmm-brain-readme.md) gives "2005 to 2023, 18 years at Innovyze". [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md) quotes the announcement of his joining in January 2008.

**His record, counted.** The [SWMM & ICM Knowledge Library (app)](swmm-icm-knowledge-library.md) catalogs his published work: 66 newsletter editions, 163 LinkedIn articles, 278 app entries and 243 GitHub repositories at its v70 [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md). The [Bob Usefulness Index (app)](bob-usefulness-index.md) quotes from it 68 publications from 1980 to 2025 and 4,398 LinkedIn comments since September 2016, and scores his usefulness to modelers at 66 of 100 on a rubric he directed; he has not yet reviewed the individual scores [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**Added 15 November 2026: in his words and others'.** In comments on a LinkedIn post Bob credits John Haestad with a year in Australia to work on the XP-EXTRAN update, and says his career would have been very different without it; Hubert Jenny recalls meeting him in Canberra in the early 1990s [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). Bob dates that stay: Canberra, November 1991 to December 1992 [S91](../sources/S91-note-q64-answers-canberra-barrels-post-link.md), the first years this page has for the row "Between SWMM 4 and CDM" in the table above. Of the program's name he says "xp-swmm was the original and we changed it to xpswmm in 1993" [S93](../sources/S93-note-xp-swmm-renamed-xpswmm-1993.md). E. R. R. Weaver, who says he helped with SWMM 3 revisions while Bob was a graduate student, credits him with adding "double pipes" for a phosphate plant [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). Bob says double pipes are what SWMM 5 calls barrels, and guesses he added them to SWMM 4 [S91](../sources/S91-note-q64-answers-canberra-barrels-post-link.md); which version it was is Q65. His LinkedIn headline that day began "SWMM since the 1977" [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md), one more start date for Q06. The post itself says he learned about SWMM at 17 and started working on it at 22, and that "SWMM 2.5 was 20,000 punch cards" [S94](../sources/S94-li-post-my-earlier-history-with-swmm.md). These are recollections; they are collected on [Anecdotes of SWMM](../concepts/swmm-anecdotes.md).

**Added 16 November 2026, from the EXTRAN and QA ingest.** Two professional facts from this batch. He compiled the five Challenge Test Cases of EPA's 2006 SWMM 5 quality assurance report while at CDM: circular conduits in series with flat slopes, pipe constrictions, steep drops, adverse slopes and inlet offsets [S185](../sources/S185-epa-swmm5-qa-report-dynamic-wave-2006.md). And in his words, he keeps this wiki "to make model comparisons and not be a writer", and he dogfoods his apps: "i do not just make a n app there is a lot of testing", with "50 years of past testing" behind it [S187](../sources/S187-note-model-comparison-purpose-and-dogfooding.md).

**Added 16 November 2026: joining MWH Soft.** The press release of Friday, January 18, 2008 announces that he joined MWH Soft's Technical Directorate as Urban Drainage Technology Leader, coming from CDM, charged with the SWMM-based line InfoSWMM and H2OMAP SWMM [S190](../sources/S190-press-mwh-soft-dickinson-joins-2008.md). In his words, "I agreed to go work at Innovyze the week of christmas, took off a few weeks went to Los Angeles after the 2nd of Janualy" [S189](../sources/S189-note-innovyze-start-christmas-2007.md); in a 2021 webinar he says "since the end of 2007" [S188](../sources/S188-talk-water-talks-icm-sim-vs-icm-swmm-2021.md). The two fit: he agreed at the end of 2007 and started in January 2008 (the years are our reading of S189). The table row above, "Product Sector Leader" in January 2008, rests on the version of the announcement printed in [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md), which differs from the release in five places (Q97). In the same webinar he gives "SWMM 5 from 2001 to 2006" [S188](../sources/S188-talk-water-talks-icm-sim-vs-icm-swmm-2021.md).
