**Positions he has taken**
- One subcatchment width for pervious and impervious subareas is wrong in principle [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md). See [Subcatchment width](../concepts/subcatchment-width.md).
- SWMM's openness is what matters most: "you have direct access to the source code so you can verify the computational algorithms" [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md).
- Lew Rossman's SWMM 5 documentation is the best software documentation in the world [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md).
- "The model is never the problem": the problem is the person who selects the data, chooses the complexity and interprets the output [S63](../sources/S63-li-edition-53-measure-of-honest-numbers.md). See [Calibration, validation and uncertainty](../concepts/calibration-validation-uncertainty.md) and Q40.
- Tacit knowledge "may be quantified, I hope" [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md).

**Disagreements between his own articles** (see [Open questions](../open-questions.md))
- Q05. [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md) says both that he led the CRADA that produced SWMM 5 and that the CDM team was led by Ted Burgess. The 2008 announcement quoted in [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md) calls him "the principal developer" of SWMM5, while [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md) and [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md) name Lew Rossman as its architect and author.
- Q06. [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md) says he has been "inside this story since 1980" and, a few lines on, working with SWMM "since 1978". The anniversary counts in [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md) were updated in different years. [S55](../sources/S55-swmm-brain-readme.md) puts his first model run in 1974; [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md) has him an undergraduate that year and on punch cards from 1977.
- Q29. [S55](../sources/S55-swmm-brain-readme.md) gives "2005 to 2023, 18 years at Innovyze". [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md) quotes the announcement of his joining in January 2008.

**Not in the wiki by rule.** Family, health and private life, though some sources mention them (CLAUDE.md, page rule 9).

**His record, counted.** The [SWMM & ICM Knowledge Library (app)](swmm-icm-knowledge-library.md) catalogs his published work: 66 newsletter editions, 163 LinkedIn articles, 278 app entries and 243 GitHub repositories at its v70 [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md). The [Bob Usefulness Index (app)](bob-usefulness-index.md) quotes from it 68 publications from 1980 to 2025 and 4,398 LinkedIn comments since September 2016, and scores his usefulness to modelers at 66 of 100 on a rubric he directed; he has not yet reviewed the individual scores [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**Added 15 November 2026: in his words and others'.** In comments on a LinkedIn post Bob credits John Haestad with a year in Australia to work on the XP-EXTRAN update, and says his career would have been very different without it; Hubert Jenny recalls meeting him in Canberra in the early 1990s [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). E. R. R. Weaver credits him with adding "double pipes" to SWMM 3 for a phosphate plant [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). His LinkedIn headline that day began "SWMM since the 1977" [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md), one more start date for Q06.
