Bob's film SWMM 1 Engine Mograph: a 60-second film of the wonders of the SWMM 1 Engine. Version 1.0, 2026-11-16 [S324](../sources/S324-swmm1-engine-mograph-app-md.md). Its `links:` line names no live Netlify address [S324](../sources/S324-swmm1-engine-mograph-app-md.md).

**Its source.** [S324](../sources/S324-swmm1-engine-mograph-app-md.md) is the technical summary the app ships with. Bob has not read this page.

**What the summary says** [S324](../sources/S324-swmm1-engine-mograph-app-md.md)
- A 60-second film, drawn by one function of time, showing five wonders of the SWMM 1 Engine, the 1971 program rebuilt from 12,200 printed cards, using only numbers taken from the app's own pages.
- Every number on screen is traced: 9 settled frames hold 43 distinct numbers, all in the film's facts file, and the block counts add to the app's 12,200. The Record button produced a 60-second, 1920 by 1080 WebM of 16.9 MB in headless Chromium.
- Block counts add to 12,200 = the app's total.
- Scene lengths add to 60 s = 1800 frames; MP4 frame count after render: 1,800.
- Trace: 9 settled frames hold 43 distinct numbers; all 43 are in facts.json or the film's own short list.
- Determinism: 18 frames drawn twice in Chromium, identical pixel for pixel.

**How to use it, as the summary puts it.** Use it as a one-minute introduction to the SWMM 1 Engine; the Record button saves it as a WebM where the browser allows. No engine runs and no person has reviewed the film; its numbers came from a text reader's account of the live page, not from its source. Cards, printout and hydrograph are drawings. [S324](../sources/S324-swmm1-engine-mograph-app-md.md)

**What it does not show.** No engine runs. Cards, printout and hydrograph are drawings; the hydrograph is a formula.
