Bob's app NetSTORM Explorer turns CDM Smith's one-page NetSTORM site, http://www.dynsystem.com/netstorm/, into a searchable map of 79 links in 10 categories and 138 release notes in 13 undated groups, read on November 18, 2026. Version 1.0.0 [S381](../sources/S381-netstorm-explorer-app-md.md). NetSTORM is CDM Smith's free Windows program for storage-treatment-overflow modeling (after HEC-STORM), rainfall IDF analysis, time series work, weather data import, and SWMM and MOUSE calibration reporting [S381](../sources/S381-netstorm-explorer-app-md.md). Its `links:` line names no Netlify or artifact address [S381](../sources/S381-netstorm-explorer-app-md.md).

**How far to trust it** [S381](../sources/S381-netstorm-explorer-app-md.md). Every entry is reworded. The reader dropped the page's link addresses: 50 were supplied by Claude from general knowledge and are unverified, and 29 links are search-only. Module and kind tags are Claude's. The page dates none of its 13 release groups. CDM Smith had no part in the app.

**An open point from the page** [S381](../sources/S381-netstorm-explorer-app-md.md): its introduction credits NetSTORM with multi-structure STORM modeling, while the release note for the C# rewrite says multi-structure modeling was omitted (Q123).
