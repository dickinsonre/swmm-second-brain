Bob's app BobSWMM App Catalog: Bob's catalog of his own small web apps (BobSWMM, also called the Multiverse Educational Lab), rebuilt from its Lovable source as one HTML file with the finder, toolkits, workflows, a map, a glossary and a Health sheet. The original lives at bobswmmapps.lovable.app. Version 1.0, 2026-10-21 [S267](../sources/S267-bobswmm-app-catalog-summary.md). Netlify address: https://bobswmm-app-catalog.netlify.app [S205](../sources/S205-note-netlify-addresses.md).

**A caution about its source.** The page ships no technical summary. [S267](../sources/S267-bobswmm-app-catalog-summary.md) was written by Claude on 17 November 2026 from the page, with nothing rerun, and Bob has not yet read it (Q108).

**What the summary says** [S267](../sources/S267-bobswmm-app-catalog-summary.md)
- 106 records and 364 wires: 88 apps on the main shelf, 6 math curiosities, and 12 older apps kept in the data but hidden.
- 18 records carry hidden: true; 6 still appear in the Math Curiosities drawer and 12 appear nowhere.
- Five hero tools show lines such as "Used 32 times this week", typed into the code under a comment reading "Simulated weekly usage counts".
- The finder expands words through a synonym table; for a conversion request, XP to SWMM Bridge, the tool built for the job, ranks 4th behind InfoSewer To ICM Guide.
- The original's single app review is marked in its source as AI-generated.
- Counts and findings are checked by a node test and an independent Python audit; every finding quotes its source lines.

**What it does not show.** No person has reviewed the findings against the live original site. [S267](../sources/S267-bobswmm-app-catalog-summary.md)

**Noted at ingest.** 88 + 6 + 12 = 106 records. The simulated usage counts are a fact about the original catalog's code that a visitor to it would not see.
