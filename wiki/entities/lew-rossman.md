Of EPA's Office of Research and Development. Author of EPANET, and architect and author of the SWMM 5 engine [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md).

- Wrote and carried the SWMM 5 engine for about twenty years [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md).
- 5.2.0 (1 February 2022) is called his final major release [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md), [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md).
- Author of the SWMM 5 Reference Manual, Volume II, Hydraulics (EPA/600/R-17/111, May 2017), the primary description of what the engine computes [S60](../sources/S60-epa-swmm5-reference-manual-vol2-hydraulics.md).
- First author of the WEFTEC 2003 paper on the redevelopment of SWMM [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).
- Appears in 213 of the 2,879 OpenSWMM threads Bob posted in, more than anyone else [S09](../sources/S09-openswmm-thread-list.md).

**How Bob describes him.** "A brilliant programmer, developer and explainer of EPANET and SWMM for decades. We all owe him a lot of thanks" [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md). His SWMM 5 manuals: "fantastic and almost a work of engineering art" [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md).

**The other side of one maintainer.** [S22](../sources/S22-li-ten-problems.md) lists "too much rested on one person" as problem 3: one queue, one set of priorities, and reasoning that never got written down. Bob's comment there credits Rossman's skill as vital to SWMM 5.

**On the walls.** The [SWMM5 Code House (app)](swmm5-code-house.md) credits him as the principal developer of EPA SWMM 5.2.4, whose source it shows unchanged [S68](../sources/S68-swmm5-code-house-app-md.md).

**In the translations.** Every SWMM5 translation README says: "its engine is Lewis Rossman's design, and this port keeps every algorithm as he wrote it" [S41](../sources/S41-swmm5-go-readme.md). See [SWMM5 language translations](swmm5-language-ports.md).
