Bob's cited library of SWMM and rainfall-runoff literature: a page where no PDF sits on the shelf without a complete citation. Made in one session on 25 October 2026 [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md).

**A caution about its source.** [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md) is the text of the page and its Doc essay, not a technical summary. It names no version, and the list of the 38 documents is not in it.

**The rule.** "Nothing joins the library without a citation." Each of the 38 records carries the citation fields, a link to where the copy came from, the license its publisher states, a short summary, and the file's SHA-256 fingerprint [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md).

**How a file is recognized.** The browser computes the SHA-256 of a dropped file and looks the number up; the file is never sent anywhere. A match opens the file with its citation. Anything else waits in a queue until five checks pass: a title, an author or organization, a four-digit year, a place where it appeared, and a well-formed link if one is given [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md).

**What is hosted.** 18 of the PDFs may be passed around freely and travel with the page. For the other 20 the reader brings a copy and the page recognizes it. Two files that might have been hosted were held back over conflicting license notices [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md).

**Printed pages are not PDF pages.** The page maps PDF pages to printed page numbers by a vote over headers and footers, so that "Cite this page" gives the printed number. 32 of the 38 documents have a page map. In the SWMM 4 file, PDF page 30 is printed page 6 [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md).

**What the checking turned up about SWMM's own manuals** [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md)
- The SWMM 4 file on Academia.edu is an excerpt: 76 pages that stop at printed page 52, of a manual that runs past page 500.
- SWMM's EPA user's manuals are dated 1971, 1975, 1981 and 1988, each an edited descendant of the one before.
- The 1988 manual was printed twice. The October 1992 second printing changed the title page, the disclaimer, the preface and pages 23, 28 to 32, 124, 220 to 221 and 375 to 377a. A page citation can land on different text in the two printings.
- The SWMM Applications Manual's title page carries a placeholder report number, EPA/600/R-09/000; EPA's Science Inventory gives EPA/600/R-09/077.

The 1988 manual is the one by Huber and Dickinson; its EXTRAN addendum is cited on [EXTRAN](extran.md) [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md). See [SWMM version history, 1969 to 2026](../concepts/swmm-version-history.md).

**Five citation styles.** APA, ASCE, Chicago, BibTeX and RIS, all drawn from one stored record [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md).

**What no person has checked.** "The 38 citations themselves, the license calls and the summaries." They are Claude's work until Bob or another reader confirms them [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md).

**For this wiki.** The page stores a PDF for others only when it is a U.S. government or Creative Commons document [S86](../sources/S86-master-swmm-pdf-page-text.md). This repository's schema likewise keeps third-party papers out of `raw/` without Bob's say. Which of the 38 documents are hosted is not in the source.
