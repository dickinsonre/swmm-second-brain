Of the University of Florida; first author of the SWMM 3 and SWMM 4 user's manuals [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).

- Had an office with [James P. (Jim) Heaney](james-heaney.md) on the fourth floor of Black Hall, where the article says SWMM was developed between 1969 and 1992 [S12](../sources/S12-li-diamond-village.md), [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).
- Bob first heard him or Jim Heaney mention SWMM in an introductory environmental engineering course at the University of Florida [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md).
- SWMM 3 User's Manual: Huber, Heaney, Nix, Dickinson and Polmann, EPA 600/2-84-109a, November 1981 [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).
- SWMM 4 User's Manual: Huber and Dickinson, August 1988 [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md).
- With Heaney, started on the stormwater software at the University of Florida in 1967, in Bob's account; Bob calls the two "a water resources modeling team at UF that complemented each other" [S64](../sources/S64-li-heaney-1982-paper-cost-allocation-app.md).
- Gave a history of SWMM at ICWMM 2020 [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md); Bob's SWMM history app is based on his ICWMM presentations [S21](../sources/S21-li-edition-56-swmm-history.md).
- Bob credits him, with Lew Rossman, for the SWMM 5 documentation, Huber for the hydrology [S11](../sources/S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md).

**Not in the sources yet.** His own account of SWMM 1 and 2. The ICWMM 2020 presentation is embedded in [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md) as a link only.
