Bob's 100-point scorecard for one question: did someone become better able to model, decide, learn or help because of what he shared? Version 2.2, 11 November 2026 [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**The rubric** [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md)
- Four pillars: solve real modeling problems, build things others can use, make difficult ideas clear, help people move forward.
- Five criteria in each, scored 0 to 5: 0 no contribution yet, 1 a useful start, 2 delivered, 3 in service, 4 confirmed useful, 5 lasting or repeatable value.
- The index is the sum of the 20 scores, with equal weights.
- An evidenced total caps at 2 any criterion whose evidence line is empty.

**The baseline: 66.** Pillars 16, 16, 16 and 18. The scores were read criterion by criterion from the [SWMM & ICM Knowledge Library (app)](swmm-icm-knowledge-library.md) at its v63, and each carries an evidence line and a gap line. Version 1.0, made on a ChatGPT site, showed an example score of 92 with no evidence [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**How the scale was set.** Version 2.0 scored 55 with level 3 read as "used by someone else". Version 2.1 reworded level 3, at Bob's direction, to "In service: delivered more than once, in a public place, and kept up", and the baseline became 66. The summary says level 3 "is easy on purpose": it counts volume and upkeep, and evidence that the work helped someone starts at level 4 [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**Highest and lowest** [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md)

| Score | Criterion | Evidence line, in brief |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Explain the mechanism | 68 publications from 1980 to 2025, among them the SWMM III and SWMM 4 user's manuals and the EXTRAN addendum |
| 5 | Give direct help | 4,398 LinkedIn comments since September 2016 and 2,744 forum answers since 1995 |
| 2 | Make it reproducible | No tool or app record carries inputs, versions and expected outputs |
| 2 | Check understanding | No reader or learner feedback is recorded anywhere in the library |

The forum figures agree with [OpenSWMM.org (the forum)](openswmm-forum.md): 2,744 answered plus 135 started is the 2,879 threads counted there [S09](../sources/S09-openswmm-thread-list.md).

**The gap it keeps naming.** "Library counts measure activity, not outcome. 2,744 forum answers is not a count of problems solved." Several gap lines ask for the same thing: one named person who ran a tool, followed an edition, or confirmed a fix [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**Who made the scores.** Claude proposed the 20 scores and wrote the 40 lines from the library's Doc essay and technical summary; it did not open the individual records. Bob set the meaning of level 3 and "has not yet reviewed the 20 scores or the 40 lines". Responsibility for every score is his [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).

**Checks.** Mechanical only: the scores add to 66; the collection counts add to the library's 9,955 at v63; 57 of 58 distinct numbers in the lines were found in the library's text [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md). The library has since moved to v70 and 9,964 records [S84](../sources/S84-swmm-icm-knowledge-library-app-md.md).

**What it is not.** No peer comparison or ranking. The equal pillar weights are a choice carried over from v1.0, not a finding [S85](../sources/S85-bob-usefulness-index-app-md.md).
