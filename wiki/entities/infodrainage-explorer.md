Bob's Replit app InfoDrainage Explorer: a personal reference and learning tool about Autodesk InfoDrainage: a release timeline, a feature comparison, a quiz, a documentation index and a facts tab. Version 1.0.0, at `bob-swmm-info.replit.app` [S110](../sources/S110-infodrainage-explorer-replit-summary.md).

**A caution about its source.** Everything on this page rests on [S110](../sources/S110-infodrainage-explorer-replit-summary.md), which Replit Agent wrote on 15 November 2026 from the project's source. Nothing here has been read by Bob (Q67).

**What the summary says** [S110](../sources/S110-infodrainage-explorer-replit-summary.md)
- It runs no drainage simulation; it displays equation strings and does not execute them.
- Counts from the data: 31 timeline releases with 109 features, 63 of them marked major; 8 comparison categories with 46 feature rows; 30 quiz questions; 10 help sections with 56 child entries.
- The footer says "53 key pages". The data holds 54 loaded section and child entries. The source does not explain the difference.
- The quiz shuffles its questions with a Fisher-Yates loop and scores answered questions as a percentage.
- The footer displays v1.0 and a last update of February 11, 2026.
- `npm run check` passed. No test suite ran.

**What it does not show.** The Autodesk Help links were not opened. The technical statements in the reference data were not checked against current Autodesk documentation. The quiz, filters and tabs were not exercised. [S110](../sources/S110-infodrainage-explorer-replit-summary.md)

**Noted at ingest.** 10 sections and 56 child entries are 66 entries, of which 54 are marked loaded (our arithmetic).
