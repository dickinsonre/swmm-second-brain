Bob's app SWMM Futurescape: a design-fiction archive of EPA SWMM in 2030, with every claim labelled. Version 2.0, 2026-10-25 [S357](../sources/S357-swmm-futurescape-summary.md). Its `links:` line names no live Netlify address [S357](../sources/S357-swmm-futurescape-summary.md).

**A caution about its source.** The page ships no technical summary. [S357](../sources/S357-swmm-futurescape-summary.md) was written by Claude on 18 November 2026 from the page, with nothing rerun, and Bob has not yet read it (Q116).

**What the summary says** [S357](../sources/S357-swmm-futurescape-summary.md)
- A design-fiction archive: the 2024 encyclopedia page on EPA SWMM, four imagined 2030 versions of it (AI-native, climate-resilient, digital twin, open science), and the counter-scenarios they leave out. Every claim carries a label saying whether you can check it today. A single-file rebuild of Bob's Lovable project; the scenario text was written 23 July 2026, which stays the factual cutoff for scenarios A to D.
- The Lovable page's box "Verified status as of July 23, 2026" made six claims; re-checked 94 days later, on 25 October 2026, three held (EPA SWMM 5.2.4 is the newest EPA release; SWMM-CAT maintenance ended 2025-03-18; CMIP7 advancing), one was superseded (OpenSWMM 6.0.0a2 as the latest alpha), and two were overstated when written (NOAA Atlas 15 "in publication"; Atlas 15 supersedes Atlas 14). The edition lists 22 changes in all: 11 content corrections, 7 code repairs, 3 additions and 1 unit conversion.
- Six status claims re-checked with their owners on 25 October 2026: 3 held, 1 superseded, 2 overstated.
- Each of the 22 edition notes gives what was there, what is there now, why, the source and a confidence.

**How to use it, as the summary puts it.** Use it to argue about where SWMM is going, with fiction and fact kept apart. The four 2030 pages are imagined; only the labelled observations can be checked, and facts in an "Observed" box have a shelf life. [S357](../sources/S357-swmm-futurescape-summary.md)

**What it does not show.** The 2030 pages are design fiction, written 23 July 2026 and not updated past that cutoff; the labels say what can be checked today, not what will happen. [S357](../sources/S357-swmm-futurescape-summary.md)
