Bob's app Chanakya's Counsel for SWMM: a decision app that walks one SWMM drainage decision through five stations taken from Kautilya's Arthashastra (Brief, Audit, Spies, Alternatives, Writ), with six measures run in EPA SWMM 5.2.4 on one sample model. Version 1.6.0, 2026-10-20 [S278](../sources/S278-chanakya-counsel-summary.md). Netlify address, from its `links:` line: https://chanakya-swmm5-swmm6.netlify.app [S278](../sources/S278-chanakya-counsel-summary.md).

**A caution about its source.** The page ships no technical summary. [S278](../sources/S278-chanakya-counsel-summary.md) was written by Claude on 18 November 2026 from the page, with nothing rerun, and Bob has not yet read it (Q111).

**What the summary says** [S278](../sources/S278-chanakya-counsel-summary.md)
- 64 engine runs of one sample model, all in the page's evidence package; three separate engine builds reproduce every packaged report exactly.
- At the 2.0 in decision storm the opening measure ("War: enlarge the pipe") gives an outfall peak of 42.3 cfs.
- With inertial damping off, storage at J3 floods 0.014 ac-ft at 2.0 in, and kinematic-wave routing nearly quadruples the base flooding.
- The page marks its pairing of statecraft and modeling as its own analogy and says the popular "three questions" saying is not in the Arthashastra.

**What it does not show.** No cost; no calibration (the meter reading is invented for teaching); one network; one two-hour storm shape; no water quality; no person has checked the quotations against the printed books; no screen reader, Safari or Firefox tried.
