A single HTML page that holds and searches Bob's conversation history with Claude, with DeepSeek and Claude Code transcripts merged in [S05](../sources/S05-swmm5org-article-facts.md). The fact sheet of 16 October 2026 calls it `claude-chat-archive.html`.

| Export parsed | 638 MB |

**Growth** [S05](../sources/S05-swmm5org-article-facts.md): 11 conversations in 2023, 164 in 2024, 429 in 2025, 1,067 in 2026 to 14 October. The busiest day was 4 May 2026 with 201 messages.

**What it has been used to measure.** [Six quality markers for app-building work](../concepts/app-quality-markers.md), and the seed of the [The SWMM findings register](../concepts/findings-register.md).

**Why searching it is not enough.** A keyword scan of the archive for defect language returned 44 conversations with unrelated apps in the top ten, so the register proposal concludes that findings "have to be read out" [S03](../sources/S03-findings-register-format.md). The same holds for this wiki: it is compiled by reading, not by search.

**Not in raw.** The archive's data (29,142,546 bytes uncompressed on 14 October 2026 [S05](../sources/S05-swmm5org-article-facts.md)) is not in this repository. Its findings come in through the register, one batch at a time.
