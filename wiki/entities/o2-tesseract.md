Bob's app O2 Tesseract: the QiYi O2 puzzle carried from its 3D toy into four dimensions and on to seventeen: in 4D, eight balls in eight windows turned by six planes, with a cycle formula for the distance of any position. Version 3.3, 2026-10-20 [S299](../sources/S299-o2-tesseract-summary.md). Netlify address, from its `links:` line: https://o2-tesseract.netlify.app [S299](../sources/S299-o2-tesseract-summary.md).

**A caution about its source.** The page ships no technical summary. [S299](../sources/S299-o2-tesseract-summary.md) was written by Claude on 18 November 2026 from the page, with nothing rerun, and Bob has not yet read it (Q111).

**What the summary says** [S299](../sources/S299-o2-tesseract-summary.md)
- In 4D there are 192 positions, never more than three moves from solved.
- Distance from solved is n - E - O/2 moves (n - E quarter turns) for E even and O odd cycles; every count and distance to 7D was computed twice by breadth-first search (Python and the page), 8D searched exhaustively, and above that the formula is used.

**What it does not show.** A model of what you can see, not a mechanism; the hidden pieces are not modelled; no person has checked the formula's case-by-case proof. [S299](../sources/S299-o2-tesseract-summary.md)
