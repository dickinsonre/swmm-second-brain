Short recollections of SWMM's history by people who were there, kept in their own words. They are memories, not documents: where one meets a manual, a listing or a dated release note, the document outranks it. Each is cited to the note it came from, and the note names who said it and where [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). The first seven come from a LinkedIn post of Bob's dated 14 November 2026 [S94](../sources/S94-li-post-my-earlier-history-with-swmm.md) and the comments under it.

**Twenty thousand punch cards.** Bob learned about SWMM at 17 and started working on it at 22; in between he "got really good at managing punch cards" [S94](../sources/S94-li-post-my-earlier-history-with-swmm.md). "SWMM 2.5 was 20,000 punch cards, so it was a beast to manage" [S94](../sources/S94-li-post-my-earlier-history-with-swmm.md). An earlier article has SWMM 2.5 and SWMM 3 "in ten boxes" [S19](../sources/S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md). A box held 2,000 cards, he recalls [S95](../sources/S95-note-punch-card-box-held-2000-cards.md), so ten boxes are 20,000 cards.

**How Bob got to XP-EXTRAN.** Bob credits John Haestad with a year in Australia "to work on the XP-EXTRAN update". Haestad, he says, "of course" was "the XP-EXTRAN reseller with Jack Cook" [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). Bob dates the stay: Canberra "from november 1991 to december 1992" [S91](../sources/S91-note-q64-answers-canberra-barrels-post-link.md). Hubert Jenny recalls meeting Bob in Canberra in the early 1990s [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). On the name: "xp-swmm was the original and we changed it to xpswmm in 1993" [S93](../sources/S93-note-xp-swmm-renamed-xpswmm-1993.md). See [Robert (Bob) Dickinson](../entities/robert-dickinson.md). The block itself: [EXTRAN](../entities/extran.md).

**AutoCAD drainage models in the late 1980s.** Bob remembers Haestad as "so inventive in the late 80s" with AutoCAD drainage models, and asks whether they were mostly the HEC series [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md).

**Double pipes for a phosphate plant.** E. R. R. Weaver started with SWMM on a Prime mainframe in the 1980s, after Bob had left punch cards, and says he "helped with SWMM3 revisions" while Bob was a graduate student at the University of Florida [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). He credits Bob: "You added the double pipes we needed in the phosphate plant" [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). A client he names as Gardinier would not have installed double 48 in HDPE pipes, he says, had the model not got "the backflows working through the ponds", and the flow reversals were the harder problem [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). Flow reversal is one of the things only [dynamic wave routing](dynamic-wave-routing.md) handles. Asked what the double pipes were, Bob answers: "double pipes are called barrels in swmm5 - i guess i added them to swmm4" [S91](../sources/S91-note-q64-answers-canberra-barrels-post-link.md). The version is his guess. Shown that the Transport block already listed barrels in the SWMM 3 manual, he adds: "transport is not extran so barrels were added later" [S92](../sources/S92-note-barrels-transport-is-not-extran.md). See Q65 in [Open questions](../open-questions.md).

**Reel tapes and card decks.** Weaver still has the reel tapes from those archives [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). Bob asked him for SWMM 3 card decks with the identifiers in columns 73 to 80, to build "a true runoff and transport 3" [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). See [Verification against printed runs](verification-against-printed-runs.md) and [EXTRAN III Workbench (app)](../entities/extran-iii-workbench.md).

**Unfinished business from 1979.** Bob hopes that with AI and SWMM6 he can "wrap up issues I remember from 1979" [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). Caleb Buahin thanks him for his "support and contributions" on SWMM6 [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md); see [OpenSWMM 6 and HydroCouple](../entities/openswmm-6-and-hydrocouple.md).

**Water that can be told what to do.** Saša Tomić: in EPANET there is "a very limited" ability to tell water what to do in a pressurized system, while "sewer rolls down the hill" [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). He started his own career on TU Delft's DUFLOW, which he calls the predecessor of SOBEK and D-HYDRO [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md).

**Not in the sources yet.** The image in the post, from early 1981. The version in which Extran first carried barrels, beyond Bob's guess of SWMM 4. See [Open questions](../open-questions.md).
