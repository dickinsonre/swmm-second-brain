**The two posts Bob named** [S134](../sources/S134-note-linkedin-two-well-received-posts.md). Ranks and rates are our arithmetic on the list [S135](../sources/S135-li-fifty-posts-with-counts.md).
- The post on [OpenSWMM 6 and HydroCouple](../entities/openswmm-6-and-hydrocouple.md) is 21st of the 50 in impressions and 2nd in reactions. It had 10.1 reactions per 1,000 impressions, 4th of the 50, where the middle value is 2.1.

Its comments are the ones kept as anecdotes of SWMM history [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md).

**What the list shows.** Being seen and being answered are different things in it. The six most seen posts each had more than 96,000 impressions and none had more than 84 reactions. The two posts with the most comments are 42nd and 48th in impressions [S135](../sources/S135-li-fifty-posts-with-counts.md). The counts are one reading on one day, and the two posts Bob named were "1w" and "2d" old, so their impressions were still rising: 5 more on each between the screenshot and the list [S134](../sources/S134-note-linkedin-two-well-received-posts.md) [S135](../sources/S135-li-fifty-posts-with-counts.md).

**Not in the sources.**
- Links and dates for the posts. The list gives ages only.
- What a usual post gets. The list stops at 6,587 impressions, so it is his most seen posts and not a baseline (Q81).
- Reposts. The screenshot shows 9 and 1 for the two posts; the list shows no repost count for either, though it shows one for 17 others (Q81).
