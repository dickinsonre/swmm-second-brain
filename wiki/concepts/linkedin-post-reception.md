Bob asked on 15 November 2026 that this wiki keep track of his better received LinkedIn posts, and named two that had drawn "pretty good responses" [S134](../sources/S134-note-linkedin-two-well-received-posts.md). This page holds what is known about them. It is two posts so far.

**The counts**, as LinkedIn showed them to Bob on 15 November 2026 [S134](../sources/S134-note-linkedin-two-well-received-posts.md)

| Post | Reactions | Comments | Reposts | Impressions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A three-slide post on [OpenSWMM 6 and HydroCouple](../entities/openswmm-6-and-hydrocouple.md) | 168 | 8 | 9 | 16,607 |
| A personal post | 165 | 29 | 1 | 8,476 |

The second slide of the first post is headed "OpenSWMM 6" and says "Street runoff can enter the sewer. A surcharged sewer can send water back to the surface." It names github.com/HydroCouple [S134](../sources/S134-note-linkedin-two-well-received-posts.md).

**Per 1,000 impressions.** This arithmetic is ours, from the counts above.

| Post | Reactions | Comments | Reposts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| OpenSWMM 6 and HydroCouple | 10.1 | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| Personal post | 19.5 | 3.4 | 0.1 |

**What the two posts show.** The reactions are nearly equal, 168 and 165. The engine post was seen about twice as often (16,607 impressions against 8,476, a ratio of 1.96) and was reposted 9 times against 1. The personal post drew 29 comments against 8, on about half the impressions. Two posts do not make a pattern, and the counts are one reading on one day [S134](../sources/S134-note-linkedin-two-well-received-posts.md).

**What the comments held.** If the personal post is the post of 14 November 2026 [S94](../sources/S94-li-post-my-earlier-history-with-swmm.md), its comments are the ones kept as anecdotes of SWMM history: four by Bob and sentences from seven other people [S90](../sources/S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md). Bob has not said that it is (Q81).

**Not in the sources.**
- The links, dates and text of the two posts.
- What a usual post of Bob's gets. Without that, "better received" has no baseline (Q81).
- Counts for any other post. A queued article of 8 June 2024, "Unhappy with some components of the LI Social Selling Index, I make up my own SSI+ Index", is the raw file most likely to hold Bob's own view of his LinkedIn numbers.
