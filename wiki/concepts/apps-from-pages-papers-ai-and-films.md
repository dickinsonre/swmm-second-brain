**The literature.** JWMM Finder holds structured summaries of all 750 papers of the Journal of Water Management Modeling, 1993 to November 2026; its keyword flags count 211 papers that say they were not calibrated and 57 that report NSE [S343](../sources/S343-jwmm-finder-summary.md). Goyen Thesis Explorer made 17 re-derivations of the thesis's table arithmetic: 11 reproduce the printed figures, 4 differ, 2 are notes, and none overturns its conclusion [S344](../sources/S344-goyen-thesis-explorer-summary.md). ICWMM Insights labels where each number came from, and 9 of its 59 archive entries are verified [S345](../sources/S345-icwmm-insights-summary.md). Catching Huber runs Bob's Google Scholar profile forward: his h-index reaches about 37 by 2031 with or without new papers [S346](../sources/S346-catching-huber-app-md.md).

**AI that checks itself.** The AI Engineering Lab's three question-answering pipelines score 13, 20 and 22 of 24 [S347](../sources/S347-ai-engineering-lab-summary.md). HydroNeural's audit found no surrogate behind its console, and of 22 DOIs in its review 8 match exactly [S348](../sources/S348-hydroneural-summary.md). Network Mentor AI's demo numbers were invented and now come from EPA SWMM 5.2.4 [S349](../sources/S349-network-mentor-ai-summary.md). Smart Search Bot checks every sentence it writes [S350](../sources/S350-smart-search-bot-summary.md). The Monday Test keeps one evidence row per claim: of 23 claims, 12 were run on a compiled engine or real script [S356](../sources/S356-the-monday-test-summary.md). SWMM Futurescape re-checked six "verified" claims 94 days later, and three held [S357](../sources/S357-swmm-futurescape-summary.md).

**Tools, films and a hub.** A companion to the EPA SWMM Applications Manual whose calculators had called a surcharged pipe 99% full [S351](../sources/S351-swmm-guide-buddy-summary.md); a web-to-Markdown converter for the library [S352](../sources/S352-info-to-mark-magic-summary.md); a repository sketcher [S353](../sources/S353-repo-sketch-art-summary.md). Two films trace every on-screen number to a facts file [S354](../sources/S354-online-library-mograph-app-md.md) [S355](../sources/S355-swmm-second-brain-mograph-app-md.md). A hub page lists Bob's organizations and counts [S358](../sources/S358-bob-dickinsons-swmm-universe-summary.md). Ommatidia Flyover's .out reader matched 8,875,800 values against the SWMM Results Kit reader with 0 wrong [S359](../sources/S359-ommatidia-flyover-summary.md).
