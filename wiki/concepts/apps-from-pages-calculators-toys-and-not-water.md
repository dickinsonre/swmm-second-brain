**Calculators rebuilt.** HydroCalc Studio's original drew its flow profile as normal depth with a 5% sine wave; the edition solves the gradually varied flow by direct step [S360](../sources/S360-hydrocalc-studio-summary.md). Ponce Hydrology Lab fixed 57 calculation defects across three versions and passes 208 unit tests [S361](../sources/S361-ponce-hydrology-lab-summary.md). Pipes & Cistern sets the exam answer beside a real tank with a hole: the exam line fills at 9 h, the real tank at 11.66 h [S365](../sources/S365-pipes-and-cistern-summary.md). Hydro City Craft's ponding no longer depends on the grid: 8.29 in at every size, where version 1.0 went from 12.60 to 9.26 in [S363](../sources/S363-hydro-city-craft-summary.md).

**Teaching toys.** St. Venant Water Flow for Grandkids corrects its original, which said SWMM5 uses the Preissmann four-point scheme [S362](../sources/S362-st-venant-water-flow-summary.md). LEGO Water Flow Lab [S364](../sources/S364-lego-water-flow-lab-summary.md), Inventing Memory [S366](../sources/S366-inventing-memory-summary.md) and Monte Carlo Area Estimator [S367](../sources/S367-monte-carlo-area-estimator-summary.md) each list the defects of their originals.

**Not about water modeling.** A dashboard of what a physics app measures about itself, where friction depends on the timestep (6.63 m of slide at 1/30 s, 0.0 m at 1/240 s) [S368](../sources/S368-convex-solids-scorecard-summary.md); Riverworld in 3D [S369](../sources/S369-riverworld-3d-app-md.md); Sisu Fire Control Drill [S370](../sources/S370-sisu-fire-control-drill-summary.md); Trireme Under Oar and Sail [S371](../sources/S371-trireme-under-oar-and-sail-summary.md); Lever Espresso Machine [S372](../sources/S372-lever-espresso-machine-summary.md); Qubit Workbench [S373](../sources/S373-qubit-workbench-summary.md); Four Degrees at the Bottom, a lake heat model [S374](../sources/S374-four-degrees-at-the-bottom-summary.md); Topology Surface Explorer [S375](../sources/S375-topology-surface-explorer-summary.md).
