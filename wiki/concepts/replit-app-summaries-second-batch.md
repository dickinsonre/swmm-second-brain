**Where they differ**
- Engines. Four run a build of EPA SWMM: seven engine entries [S117](../sources/S117-batchswmm56-replit-summary.md), one EPA reference with 30 imported translations [S119](../sources/S119-code-rosetta-stone-replit-summary.md), 5.1.14 as WebAssembly [S123](../sources/S123-swmm5-modeler-replit-summary.md), 5.1.015 beside EPANET [S128](../sources/S128-epanet-23-generator-replit-summary.md). One runs SWMM5 and EPANET through PySWMM and WNTR [S120](../sources/S120-forcemain-swmm5-epanet-comparison-replit-summary.md). One routes with its own Ruby code [S125](../sources/S125-kinematic-wave-router-replit-summary.md). One ships the 5.2.4 source with a stub for an engine [S116](../sources/S116-swmm5-explorer-replit-summary.md). The other ten give `engines: none`.
- Tests. Passing: 33 API and 12 subset tests [S119](../sources/S119-code-rosetta-stone-replit-summary.md), 5 [S120](../sources/S120-forcemain-swmm5-epanet-comparison-replit-summary.md), 15 [S126](../sources/S126-swmm-ecosystem-replit-summary.md), 9 [S128](../sources/S128-epanet-23-generator-replit-summary.md). Partly: 549 of 550 [S117](../sources/S117-batchswmm56-replit-summary.md), 9 of 10 built-in checks [S115](../sources/S115-epanet-for-swmm5-experts-replit-summary.md). Failing: zero passed and eight failed [S125](../sources/S125-kinematic-wave-router-replit-summary.md). The other ten have no suite.
- Five fail their TypeScript check: [S114](../sources/S114-swmm5-microgpt-replit-summary.md), [S123](../sources/S123-swmm5-modeler-replit-summary.md), [S124](../sources/S124-bobswmm-auto-research-replit-summary.md), [S127](../sources/S127-icm-infoworks-release-history-replit-summary.md), [S129](../sources/S129-historical-water-inventions-replit-summary.md).
- Ten call an AI model when they run: [S116](../sources/S116-swmm5-explorer-replit-summary.md), [S117](../sources/S117-batchswmm56-replit-summary.md), [S119](../sources/S119-code-rosetta-stone-replit-summary.md), [S120](../sources/S120-forcemain-swmm5-epanet-comparison-replit-summary.md), [S121](../sources/S121-hindsight-prophet-replit-summary.md), [S124](../sources/S124-bobswmm-auto-research-replit-summary.md), [S126](../sources/S126-swmm-ecosystem-replit-summary.md), [S127](../sources/S127-icm-infoworks-release-history-replit-summary.md), [S128](../sources/S128-epanet-23-generator-replit-summary.md), [S129](../sources/S129-historical-water-inventions-replit-summary.md). One trains its own small GPT in the browser [S114](../sources/S114-swmm5-microgpt-replit-summary.md).
