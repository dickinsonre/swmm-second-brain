**Claims worth keeping** (all copied from the file)
- A 60-second film, drawn by one function of time, showing five wonders of the SWMM 1 Engine, the 1971 program rebuilt from 12,200 printed cards, using only numbers taken from the app's own pages.
- Every number on screen is traced: 9 settled frames hold 43 distinct numbers, all in the film's facts file, and the block counts add to the app's 12,200. The Record button produced a 60-second, 1920 by 1080 WebM of 16.9 MB in headless Chromium.
- Block counts add to 12,200 = the app's total.
- Scene lengths add to 60 s = 1800 frames; MP4 frame count after render: 1,800.
- Trace: 9 settled frames hold 43 distinct numbers; all 43 are in facts.json or the film's own short list.
- Determinism: 18 frames drawn twice in Chromium, identical pixel for pixel.

**Limits it states.** No engine runs. Cards, printout and hydrograph are drawings; the hydrograph is a formula. Numbers came from a text reader's account of the live page (read twice), not from the app's source. An error on the page or in the reading is repeated here. The app's screen layout is not shown; Claude never saw it. The film inherits the app's limits: 10,889 retyped cards not read by a person, 17 doubtful readings, single precision (3 to 4 digits, not 6). Record button: exercised once in headless Chromium, where it produced a 60-second, 1920 by 1080 WebM of 16.9 MB. It has not been tried in a person's browser or inside the claude.ai viewer, where a save needs the viewer's permission.
