**What it is.** Bob's own account of his years with SWMM 2.5, 3, 4, 5, XP-SWMM, Visual SWMM and InfoSWMM, first published in 2016 and amended in 2017, 2018 and 2023. Raw file: [raw/linkedin/2016-11-18-my-history-various-versions-swmm-swmm3-swmm4-swmm5-robert-dickinson.md](../../raw/linkedin/2016-11-18-my-history-various-versions-swmm-swmm3-swmm4-swmm5-robert-dickinson.md).

**How it was written.** By Bob, with two quoted company texts: a 2003 CDM profile and Paul Boulos's January 2008 announcement. Some paragraphs read as later AI polish; the article does not say. The anniversary counts were updated in different years and do not all share one base year.

**Claims worth keeping**
- He first read about SWMM at 17, as a high-school senior, in a University of Florida brochure.
- SWMM 3, SWMM 4 and SWMM 5 are public domain with open source code.
- InfoSWMM is an ArcGIS extension that uses the SWMM 5 C code as its engine.
- He joined CDM in late 2000 and worked on the SWMM 5 upgrade with Lew Rossman and Trent Schade at EPA and CDM colleagues Jim Smullen, Ted Burgess, Carl Chan, Charles Rowney and Khalid Khan.
- At CDM he added population and pump curves for Miami-Dade and flow regulators for Winnipeg, and snowmelt, statistics and diurnal patterns for ALCOSAN in Pittsburgh.
- He joined MWH Soft (later Innovyze) in January 2008 as Product Sector Leader for InfoSWMM, H2OMAP SWMM, InfoSewer and H2OMap Sewer.
- Degrees from the University of Florida in environmental engineering: B.E. 1979 and M.E. 1985.
- He calls Lew Rossman's SWMM 5 documentation "the best software documentation in the world".
