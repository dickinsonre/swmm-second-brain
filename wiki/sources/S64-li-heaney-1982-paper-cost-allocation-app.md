**Claims worth keeping**
- Bob first met Jim Heaney in 1976 at the University of Florida, where Heaney taught a water resources class, and began working with him in 1977 on applying game theory to water resources.
- The 1977 work led to the paper: James P. Heaney and Robert E. Dickinson, "Methods for Apportioning the Cost of a Water Resource Project", Water Resources Research, Vol. 18, No. 3, pp. 476 to 482, June 1982. Bob says Heaney wrote it and made him co-author.
- The paper compared the Shapley value, the core, the nucleolus and SCRB, and proposed MCRS (Minimum Costs, Remaining Savings). Its contribution, as the article puts it: MCRS uses the actual bounds of the core, not nominal bounds, so the allocation lies in the stable region.
- The worked example: three towns (Riverside, Hilltop, Lakeview) share a reservoir; $12 million built separately, $7 million together, $5 million saved.
- The app, at heaneywrr1982.lovable.app, as of March 2026: four methods (SCRB, Shapley value, Nucleolus, Equal Split) for 3 or 4 players; a core plot in barycentric coordinates with draggable points; step-by-step calculations including all 6 Shapley orderings; five chart modes; a Story Mode; quizzes; a glossary; scenarios (Three Towns, airport runway, factory pollution, an empty-core game). AI-written passage, not checked against the code.
- Heaney was a co-author on SWMM 1, SWMM 2 and SWMM 3; he and Wayne Huber started on the stormwater software at the University of Florida in 1967.
- A line from Wayne Huber's memorial tribute, as quoted: Heaney "was one of the earliest to become captivated by the possibility of Lotus 1-2-3 for solving engineering problems". Bob adds that Heaney built early expert systems in spreadsheets in the 1980s.
- "Jim's Google Scholar page lists 253 citations"; his most-cited paper, "Robust water system design with commercial intelligent search optimizers" (1999), has 144. AI-written passage, not checked (Q37).
- The Shapley value dates from 1953; SHAP (Scott Lundberg and Su-In Lee) applies it to explaining machine-learning predictions. AI-written passage, not checked.
- Bob co-authored the SWMM 3 manual with Heaney and Huber and the SWMM 4 manual with Huber.
