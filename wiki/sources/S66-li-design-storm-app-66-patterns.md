**Claims worth keeping**
- The app in February 2026: 66 patterns, 9 regional tabs, more than 30 countries, 8 export formats, a three-step workflow (storm parameters, pattern, review and export). Free, in the browser, no login, at rain-canvas-studio.lovable.app.
- Why a pattern matters: five inches over 24 hours spread evenly is a drizzle; the same five inches in a two-hour burst is not. The total is the same and the pipes needed are not.
- The four SCS types (I, IA, II, III) are published in TR-55, 1986, and are little used outside the United States.
- The nine tabs: US Agency, UK and ICM, European, Asian, GCC, African, Latin American, International, Simple.
- The eight formats: CSV, SWMM5 `.dat`, InfoWorks ICM, JSON, PNG, SVG, PDF, animated GIF. Every exported file carries a header with depth, duration, pattern, peak intensity and timestamp.
- Other tools named: pattern comparison with R-squared and root mean square error; a China IDF calculator for 279 cities; a Canada IDF calculator for 26 cities; sensitivity analysis; a pattern derivation engine; historical storm matching against all 66 patterns; a Real Data Hub that parses uploaded NOAA, CSV, SWMM `.inp` and HEC gage files in the browser.
- Listed as coming: live NOAA Atlas 14 lookup, return period as an input, HEC-HMS export, shareable URLs, PDF engineering reports, a regulatory acceptance matrix.
- Two unmeasured claims: building a rainfall file falls from "twenty minutes" to "forty-five seconds"; "the first free web tool" to offer a Saudi MOMRA distribution. AI-written passage, not checked.

**Against the later summary.** [S65](S65-world-rainfall-pattern-painter-app-md.md), nine months on, counts 339 patterns and grades 279 of them Low confidence. This article gives no confidence level for any of its 66. It lists HEC-HMS export as coming, while the change list in S65 dates an HEC-HMS gage export to version 1.2.0, 3 December 2025 (Q42).
