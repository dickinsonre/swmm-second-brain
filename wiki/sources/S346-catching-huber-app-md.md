**Claims worth keeping** (all copied from the file)
- A ledger and simulator that grades Bob Dickinson's publication record against Wayne Huber's and runs his Google Scholar profile forward year by year, with a named plan of 26 papers and without it.
- From 5,683 citations, an h-index of 33 and an i10-index of 76, the h-index reaches about 37 by 2031 with or without new papers; the two runs first differ in 2033, and in 2036 only four new papers, all reference papers, are in the h-core. The stand-in list of 130 counts holds only seven real counts, and the growth rule overshoots the measured since-2021 h by 3, so the gain is an upper estimate.
- h-index on four hand-worked lists: 4, 3, 0, 0.
- Huber's top-20 OpenAlex list: h = 13, equal to OpenAlex's stated 13.
- Stand-in list: 130 entries, total 5,683, h 33, i10 76.
- Null control (G = 0, no papers): every year 5,683 / 33 / 76 / 130 items.

**Limits it states.** Huber's Google Scholar citations or h-index. None found. Target h 40 is a placeholder. Bob's OpenAlex or Scopus record. Not looked up. Bob's real per-work counts. The list and the 130-item count are stand-ins. How many of the 76 works are peer-reviewed journal papers. Acceptance, publication date or real citation rate of any planned paper. The three rates are assumptions. The growth rule overshoots the measured since-2021 h by 3 (check 9), so the existing-works h gain (33 to 37 by 2031) is an upper estimate. Self-citation, unmerged duplicates, or changes in Scholar's rules. Whether the model corpus can be released; licenses are unchecked.
