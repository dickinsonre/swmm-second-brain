**What it is.** A table of 50 of Bob's LinkedIn posts with the reactions, comments and impressions LinkedIn showed for each on 15 November 2026. Raw file: [raw/linkedin/2026-11-15-fifty-posts-with-counts.md](../../raw/linkedin/2026-11-15-fifty-posts-with-counts.md).

**How it was written.** The posts are Bob's. He copied a list of them from LinkedIn and attached the text to a Claude session on 15 November 2026. Claude (Anthropic) read the whole paste and typed the raw file from it with a script: each post's place, age, first twelve words, attached title and counts. The full text of the posts is not in the repository, because it names many people and gives an email address and the repository is public. Bob has not read the file.

**What is left out of the wiki.** One of the 50 is the post of 14 November 2026, much of which is about Bob's family. By page rule 9 the wiki takes its counts and nothing else; [S94](S94-li-post-my-earlier-history-with-swmm.md) is its page.

**Claims worth keeping**
- The paste held 50 posts in descending order of impressions, from 164,528 down to 6,587. Their ages run from "2d" to "1yr".
- The most seen, a post beginning "More amazing SWMM6 alpha news from the Open SWMM Group", had 164,528 impressions, 48 reactions and 4 comments.
- The most reactions, 354, went to a post beginning "A webinar about #SWMM6", which had 44,858 impressions and 5 comments.
- A post beginning "What is #HydroCouple for 1D and 2D stormwater modeling?" was "1w" old and had 16,612 impressions, 168 reactions and 8 comments. Its text calls its picture a GIF.
- A post beginning "Since I write about SWMM here so often" was "2d" old and had 8,481 impressions, 165 reactions and 29 comments. No other post in the list has more comments.
- The next most comments, 21, went to a post beginning "This is a simple app about design storms around the world", with 6,793 impressions and 52 reactions.
- A post beginning "I was getting tired of the AI constantly giving me SWMM5 St Venant solution equations" had 142 reactions and 9,866 impressions; a post beginning "Design rainfall patterns or hyetographs around the world" had 134 reactions and 8,941 impressions.
- 17 of the 50 show a repost count.

**Limits.** The paste does not say which LinkedIn page it came from, what period it covers or how the 50 were chosen. It holds no dates, only ages, and no links to the posts. Posts with fewer than 6,587 impressions are not in it. The counts are one reading on one day.
