**What it is.** The technical summary carried inside the Bob Usefulness Index, version 2.2: a 100-point scorecard of how useful Bob's shared work is to water modelers, scored against the record in his Knowledge Library. Raw file: [raw/apps/bob-usefulness-index-v2.2.md](../../raw/apps/bob-usefulness-index-v2.2.md). Extracted byte for byte from Bob's artifact on 14 November 2026.

**How it was written.** By Claude (Anthropic) in a session in November 2026 under Bob's direction. Version 1.0 was published from a ChatGPT site; its four pillars, twenty criteria and equal weights are kept. Claude proposed the 20 baseline scores and wrote the 40 evidence and gap lines. "Robert set the meaning of level 3. He has not yet reviewed the 20 scores or the 40 lines."

**Claims worth keeping**
- Four pillars of five criteria, each scored 0 to 5. Baseline 16 + 16 + 16 + 18 = 66 (Solve, Reuse, Teach, Help). The v1.0 example score was 92 with no evidence.
- An evidenced total caps any criterion with an empty evidence line at 2.
- Level 3 was reworded in 2.1 from "Used" to "In service: delivered more than once, in a public place, and kept up"; the baseline was rescored from 55 to 66.
- The evidence is the Knowledge Library at v63, 11 November 2026, 9,955 records.
- Counts it quotes from the library: 2,744 forum threads answered and 135 started on openswmm.org from February 1995, 291 in the peak year 2019; 4,398 LinkedIn comments since September 2016, 670 of them replies inside a thread; 68 publications from 1980 to 2025, among them the SWMM III and SWMM 4 user's manuals and the EXTRAN addendum; 6 papers by other people cite them; 29 of 77 paper records not yet confirmed at source.
- Forum answers, comments and started threads are 73 percent of the library's records.
- swmm5.org shows 19 posts so far in 2026 against 46 in 2025.
- 15 SWMMBobSWMM6 repositories were still empty on 9 November 2026 (3 SWMM5 ports and 12 OpenSWMM 6).
- ChatGPT reviewed the library on 10 November 2026 and gave it 80 of 100; four of its five deductions have a recorded fix.
- Lowest scores, at 2: "Make it reproducible" and "Check understanding".

**Limits it states.** The scores are Claude's reading of the rubric against the library's summary of itself; Claude did not open the 9,955 individual records. "Library counts measure activity, not outcome. 2,744 forum answers is not a count of problems solved." The evidenced total checks that an evidence line exists, not that it is true. No peer comparison or ranking.

**Checked at ingest.** 2,744 + 135 = 2,879, the thread count in [S09](S09-openswmm-thread-list.md); the three thread partners' counts match it too. 16 + 16 + 16 + 18 = 66.
