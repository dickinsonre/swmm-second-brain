**What it is.** A CSV of every openswmm.org thread Bob posted in: date, title, his role, his post count, total posts, who started it, participants, URL. Raw file: [raw/openswmm/threads-1995-2026.csv](../../raw/openswmm/threads-1995-2026.csv). Titles only; the 20 MB full-text export is not in `raw/` yet.

**How it was written.** Exported from openswmm.org pages. The counts below were computed from the file on 14 November 2026 by Claude with a short script.

**Claims worth keeping**
- 2,879 threads, from 15 February 1995 ("Interface files", started by Bill James) to 24 October 2026.
- Bob's posts sum to 4,014 over the 2,872 threads that show a count; 7 threads show no posts on the page. All posts in those threads sum to 12,443.
- He started 135 threads and replied in 2,744.
- By decade: 1990s 142 threads, 2000s 691, 2010s 987, 2020s 1,059. The busiest years were 2019 (291), 2018 (220) and 2020 (212).
- 1,357 different people started the threads. The people who share the most threads with him: Lew Rossman 213, Mitchell Heineman 201, Michael Gregory 187, Peter Klaver 128, Rob James 111, Joseph Pang 100.
