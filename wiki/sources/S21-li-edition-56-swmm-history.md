**What it is.** A history of SWMM from 1969 to 2026, written to introduce Bob's SWMM history app on Replit. Raw file: [raw/linkedin/2026-03-02-edition-56-350000-model-built-billion-dollar-55-years-dickinson-choic.md](../../raw/linkedin/2026-03-02-edition-56-350000-model-built-billion-dollar-55-years-dickinson-choic.md).

**How it was written.** The article says the app's contents are "based on Wayne Huber presentations at ICWMM and a lot of AI searching and discussion with me". The essay switches between first and third person. Several of its statements disagree with primary sources in this wiki; see [Open questions](../open-questions.md).

**Claims worth keeping**
- SWMM began in 1969. The first version cost $350,000.
- Version dates: SWMM 1 1971, SWMM 2 1975, SWMM 3 1981, SWMM 4 1988, then no major version for sixteen years.
- In 2001 EPA and CDM signed a Cooperative Research and Development Agreement to rewrite SWMM. Lewis A. Rossman of EPA was the architect. SWMM 5.0.001 was released in October 2004, in C.
- SWMM 5 releases it lists: 5.0.005 (2005, first FEMA-approved version), 5.0.019 (2010, LID controls), 5.1.001 (2014), 5.1.013 (2018), 5.2.0 (2022, called Rossman's final major release), 5.2.4 (2023).
- It says SWMM 2 added EXTRAN, and that SWMM 1 was built by the University of Florida, Camp Dresser & McKee and Metcalf & Eddy.
- It names Ben Hodges (UT Austin) and A. Charles Rowney for SWMM 5+, and Caleb Buahin as lead developer of HydroCouple.
