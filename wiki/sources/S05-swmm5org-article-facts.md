**What it is.** Counts measured from Bob's Claude chat archive as rebuilt on 14 October 2026, for use in swmm5.org articles. Raw file: [raw/swmm5org/article-facts-2026-10-16.md](../../raw/swmm5org/article-facts-2026-10-16.md).

**How it was written.** By Claude (it names Opus 5), which wrote the extractor and the six marker patterns. Sections 1, 2 and 6 are counts. Section 3 depends on the printed patterns. Section 5 is copied from Bob's notes and not re-verified.

**Claims worth keeping**
- The archive held 1,671 conversations, 17,991 messages, 533 app-building conversations and 800 distinct apps, from 21 November 2023 to 14 October 2026.
- By year: 2023, 11 conversations; 2024, 164; 2025, 429; 2026 to 14 October, 1,067 with 779 apps. May 2026 was the peak month: 213 conversations, 147 apps.
- Six quality markers per quarter. From 2025Q4 to 2026Q3: runs a real engine 29 % to 62 %; verified against engine output 29 % to 60 %; reports N of N test results 7 % to 72 %; found a defect 0 % to 47 %; review rounds 0 % to 27 %; negative result 0 % to 15 %.
- A first pattern for "found a defect" read 73 % for 2026Q3; tightened after reading the matches, it read 47 %.
- 2024 and 2025 app counts are a floor, because those conversations rarely wrote files.
