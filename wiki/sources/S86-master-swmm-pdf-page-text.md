**What it is.** The text of the Master SWMM PDF app page saved as markdown: its interface labels, then its Doc essay, "Every PDF Carries Its Papers". It is not a technical summary on the app-md form and has no front matter or version. Raw file: [raw/apps/Master SWMM PDF.md](../../raw/apps/Master%20SWMM%20PDF.md). Bob uploaded it on 14 November 2026.

**How it was written.** The essay says the page and essay were made with Claude (Anthropic) in one session on 25 October 2026 under Bob's direction. Bob supplied the files and the rule that every PDF carries a citation. Claude ran four research passes against Crossref, publisher and agency pages and Academia.edu, decided what could be hosted, and wrote the summaries. "No person has yet checked the citations, the license calls or the summaries."

**Claims worth keeping**
- The library holds 38 documents on SWMM and rainfall-runoff modeling, from the 1983 USGS manual for PRMS to papers of 2022.
- Each record ties a citation to a file by its SHA-256, computed in the browser. A file that does not match waits in a queue until five checks pass: a title, an author or organization, a four-digit year, a place of publication, and a link that starts with `http` if one is given.
- 18 PDFs travel with the page because their licenses allow it; the other 20 need the reader's own copy.
- 25 DOIs were resolved against Crossref or the publisher; 25 Academia.edu pages were opened and matched.
- Printed page numbers are found by a vote over page headers and footers. 32 of the 38 documents have a page map. For 14 with a published page range, 12 agreed.
- The SWMM 4 file from Academia.edu is an excerpt: 76 pages, front matter to page xxiv, text to page 52, of a manual that runs past page 500. PDF page 30 is printed page 6.
- SWMM's EPA user's manuals are dated 1971, 1975, 1981 and 1988. The 1988 manual was printed twice; the October 1992 second printing changed the title page, the disclaimer, the preface and pages 23, 28 to 32, 124, 220 to 221 and 375 to 377a.
- The SWMM Applications Manual's title page carries the placeholder EPA/600/R-09/000; EPA's Science Inventory gives EPA/600/R-09/077.
- Citations are drawn in five styles: APA, ASCE, Chicago, BibTeX and RIS.

**Limits it states.** A re-saved or re-scanned copy will not match its fingerprint. Licenses are Claude's reading of what each publisher states; two conflicts were set aside and neither file is hosted. 13 records have no Academia.edu link. The summaries describe and do not review.
