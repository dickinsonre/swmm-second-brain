**What it is.** MWH Soft's press release announcing that Bob had joined the company, carried by WEBWIRE on Friday, January 18, 2008. Raw file: [raw/press/2008-01-18-mwh-soft-dickinson-joins.md](../../raw/press/2008-01-18-mwh-soft-dickinson-joins.md). Bob supplied the text on 16 November 2026.

**How it was written.** By MWH Soft, with quotations from its President and COO, Paul Boulos, and from Bob. It is the primary document for the announcement that Bob's 2016 article quotes ([S11](S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md)).

**Claims worth keeping**
- Bob joined MWH Soft's Technical Directorate as Urban Drainage Technology Leader, coming from CDM.
- He was charged with maintaining, enhancing and supporting the SWMM-based product line, named as InfoSWMM and H2OMAP SWMM, and with research and technology transfer with universities and government organizations.
- It credits him with "over thirty years of experience" and calls him "A principal developer of the industry-standard and FEMA-certified SWMM software", with a "key role in the USEPA Storm Water Management Model (SWMM5) redevelopment program".
- He holds an M.E. in Environmental Engineering from the University of Florida.
- Bob, quoted: "For the past five years, I have followed the company's rapid growth".

**Against the version in S11.** The announcement as [S11](S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md) prints it is not this text. It says "In January 2008, Innovyze welcomed" him "as its Product Sector Leader" (the release: MWH Soft, Urban Drainage Technology Leader); "over 35 years" (the release: over thirty); "the principal developer of the industry-standard and FEMA-certified SWMM5 software" (the release: "A principal developer of the ... SWMM software"); and adds InfoSewer and H2OMap Sewer to the product line (the release names InfoSWMM and H2OMAP SWMM). S11's version reads as a later rewrite. Q97.
