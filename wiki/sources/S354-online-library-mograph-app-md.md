**Claims worth keeping** (all copied from the file)
- A 60-second film, drawn by one function of time, showing five wonders of Robert Dickinson's SWMM & ICM Knowledge Library: one HTML file of 9,889 catalog records and 2,909 links that reaches back to 1980.
- Every number on screen is traced: 8 settled frames hold 39 distinct numbers, all found in the film's facts file; the 25 kinds of record add to 9,889, and the 135 threads started and 2,744 answered add to the 2,879 OpenSWMM.org threads. 18 frames drawn twice are identical pixel for pixel.
- The 25 kinds of record in `facts.json` add to 9,889; the threads started and answered (135 and 2,744) add to the 2,879 OpenSWMM.org threads; and the 17 smaller kinds add to the 633 shown as one bar.
- Scene lengths add to 60 s = 1800 frames; MP4 frame count after render: 1,800.
- Trace: 8 settled frames hold 39 distinct numbers; all 39 are in facts.json or the film's own short list.
- Determinism: 18 frames drawn twice in Chromium, identical pixel for pixel.

**Limits it states.** No library record is shown. The shelf, cards and search query are drawings. Counts are from the library's chart (export of 20 October 2026); its home page shows a slightly different snapshot from 1 November 2026. The film inherits the library's limits: catalog records and links, not full text; 2,909 links not rechecked by a person; the assistant's answers unreviewed. Numbers came from a text reader's account of the live page (read twice), not from its source. An error on the page or in the reading is repeated here. The page's own layout is not shown; Claude never saw it. Record button: not exercised for this film.
