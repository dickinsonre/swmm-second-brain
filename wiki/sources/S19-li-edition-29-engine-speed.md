**What it is.** A newsletter edition that times one test model on a 1987 PC and on a 2024 PC, with notes on punch cards, magnetic tapes and floppy disks. Raw file: [raw/linkedin/2024-04-28-edition-29-from-1988-2024-swmms-incredible-54000-fold-dickinson-9chye.md](../../raw/linkedin/2024-04-28-edition-29-from-1988-2024-swmms-incredible-54000-fold-dickinson-9chye.md).

**How it was written.** By Bob from memory and a 2024 run; the section summary at the top reads as AI-written. The text says "ICM AT" in several places and "IBM PC AT" in section 6.

**Claims worth keeping**
- The test model came from EXTRAN 3 in 1981, was changed to run in SWMM 4 in 1987 and 1988, became part of the SWMM 5 tests and is in the OWA-Extran set as Extran1.INP.
- In 1987 and 1988 a compile took 5 minutes with a two-pass Fortran compiler and the model, 6 hours of simulated time, took another 5 minutes to run.
- In 2024, on a 32-core PC, SWMM 5 ran a repeating version of the model from 1 January 1988 to 1 January 2024 in 5 minutes. The article gives that span as 324,360 hours and the ratio as 54,060.
- Bob used punch cards for SWMM from 1977 to 1980. SWMM 2.5 and SWMM 3 filled ten boxes of cards. SWMM 3 was kept on magnetic tape from 1980 to 1990. SWMM 4 was mailed on 5 1/4 inch floppy disks.
- It lists the SWMM 3 manual (Huber, Heaney, Nix, Dickinson and Polmann, November 1981), the SWMM 4 manual (Huber and Dickinson, August 1988), the EXTRAN addendum (Roesner, Aldrich and Dickinson, August 1988) and the WEFTEC 2003 paper on the redevelopment of SWMM (Rossman, Dickinson, Schade, Chan, Burgess, Lai and Sullivan).
