**Claims worth keeping**
- Bob learned about SWMM when he was 17.
- He started working on SWMM when he was 22.
- At 20 and 21 he learned programming, "or at least got really good at managing punch cards".
- "SWMM 2.5 was 20,000 punch cards, so it was a beast to manage."
- The post's image is "from early 1981 in the days of SWMM3".
- He calls it "a lucky accident" that he came to SWMM at all and can still help with SWMM6.

**Checked at ingest.**
- Age 17 agrees with [S11](S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md), which has him reading about SWMM at 17 as a high-school senior.
- Age 22 against the start dates of Q06. [S89](S89-note-corpus-name.md) has him 75 in 2030, which puts age 22 between 1976 and 1978. That fits 1977 and 1978 and does not fit 1974 or 1980.
- 20,000 cards against [S19](S19-li-edition-29-engine-speed.md), where "SWMM 2.5 and SWMM 3 were in ten boxes". Bob has since said a box held 2,000 cards [S95](S95-note-punch-card-box-held-2000-cards.md), so ten boxes are 20,000 cards and the two figures agree (Q66, ticked).
- SWMM 1 was 12,200 cards in the 1971 listing [S01](S01-swmm1-engine-app-md.md). The post's 20,000 for SWMM 2.5 is a round figure from memory, not a count of a listing.
