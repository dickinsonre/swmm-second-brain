**What it is.** One sentence from Bob on the name of the XP Software program. Raw file: [raw/notes/xp-swmm-renamed-xpswmm-1993.md](../../raw/notes/xp-swmm-renamed-xpswmm-1993.md).

**How it was written.** Bob typed the sentence in a Claude session on 15 November 2026, after Q64 asked whether "XSWMM" in his LinkedIn comment [S90](S90-note-swmm-anecdotes-linkedin-comments.md) is XP-SWMM. Claude (Anthropic) copied it into the note in his words. The note marks two things as Claude's reading: that "we" is XP Software, and that "XSWMM" in the comment is this program with a letter dropped. Bob has not yet read the note.

**Claims worth keeping**
- "xp-swmm was the original and we changed it to xpswmm in 1993".

**Checked at ingest.** 1993 follows the stay in Canberra, November 1991 to December 1992 [S91](S91-note-q64-answers-canberra-barrels-post-link.md), by one year. No other page dates the name. His LinkedIn headline in S90 writes "XPSWMM"; his article [S11](S11-li-my-history-with-swmm.md), first published in 2016, writes both "XP-SWMM" and "XPSWMM". This wiki keeps the spelling of whichever source it is citing.
