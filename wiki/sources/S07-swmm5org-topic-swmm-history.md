**What it is.** The first of the six topic pages: posts, apps, examples and repositories on SWMM's history. Raw file: [raw/swmm5org/topics/01-swmm-history.md](../../raw/swmm5org/topics/01-swmm-history.md).

**How it was written.** Drafted by Claude in November 2026 from Bob's posts, Netlify apps and repositories; Bob chose the topics and layout.

**Claims worth keeping**
- SWMM began in 1969 to 1971 as an EPA project with Metcalf & Eddy, the University of Florida and Water Resources Engineers.
- Bob went to Florida in 1973 to work on it and has worked on SWMM itself since 1978, starting with SWMM 2.5.
- SWMM 1 and EXTRAN III were rebuilt from their printed program listings, and SWMM 4.4H from its Fortran source, so each runs in a browser.
- The page describes the SWMM 1 Engine app as "the 1971 SWMM 1 Runoff engine", which is the app as it stood before version 2.0.
- The SWMM 4.4H Fortran source is at SWMMEnablement/WCH_2004_SWMM44H.
