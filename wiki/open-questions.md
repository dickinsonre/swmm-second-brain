A queued article of 1 March 2019, [New Engine and GUI Features in InfoSWMM 14.7 which is based on EPA SWMM 5.1.013](../raw/linkedin/2019-03-01-new-engine-gui-features-infoswmm-147-which-based-epa-swmm-dickinson.md), shows the Slot option and fits 2018.

- [x] Q12 (2026-11-14): Is SWMM 5's dynamic wave solution implicit or explicit? [S20](sources/S20-li-edition-54-two-solvers.md) calls the EXTRAN lineage "implicit backward Euler time integration" with successive relaxation. [S08](sources/S08-swmm5org-topic-inside-swmm5.md) lists an app on "why SWMM5's explicit dynamic wave solution stays robust" and a 2013 post on successive under-relaxation. The 2013 post and the 2006 QA report, both listed in [S08](sources/S08-swmm5org-topic-inside-swmm5.md) but not in raw, should settle the wording. **Settled 2026-11-14 by [S60](sources/S60-epa-swmm5-reference-manual-vol2-hydraulics.md):** both. SWMM 5's step is implicit backward Euler, solved by Picard iteration, but one conduit and node at a time with no spatial coupling, so the Courant limit still applies. The versions before SWMM 5 were explicit.
